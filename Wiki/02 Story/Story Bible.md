# Story Bible

> [!spoiler] Spoiler warning
> This page reveals the truth behind the game. Do not link it from the player wiki.

## Logline

Ten years after nine guests vanished from Blackwood Lodge, the detectives who closed the case are lured back by a letter. The storm cuts the road. The host is missing. One of the guests is already dead — staged as a ritual tableau. The killer is among the investigators, and the cult that was never caught is waiting for the tenth guest.

## The truth in one page

- **1926:** Elias Blackwood inherits the lodge and funds an expedition into the Ashcombe woods. The expedition finds something the cult calls **the Hollow** — a place, a presence, or a story that grants power to those who complete the rite. Elias builds the chapel over it.
- **1936:** Nine guests are invited for a weekend. They are the cult's first attempt at the **Ninth Rite**: nine souls, a witness, and a word. The rite fails. Eight guests die; one survives. The town closes the case. The survivor is **Elias Blackwood**.
- **1936–1946:** Elias rebuilds the cult as **The Hollow Court**. He learns the rite failed because it needed ten souls, not nine, and a witness who chose to stay. He waits for the right ten.
- **1946:** **Madame Vesper**, a cult defector and fortune teller, sends a letter to the four detectives who handled the original case. She tells them the case was a lie and that the lodge is still active.
- **1947:** The investigators arrive on the anniversary. The storm is not an accident; the cult has been seeding it for weeks. The road washes out. The host — Elias — greets them, then vanishes. The first body is a VIP who funded the original expedition; the killer staged the scene to look like the old rite.
- **Tonight:** The cult needs ten souls to finish the Ninth Rite. The investigators are the ten. The killer is the cultist planted among them. The investigators' only way out is to solve the four tasks, perform the **Revealing Ritual**, speak the **Hollow Word**, and escape before dawn.
- **The twist:** The Revealing Ritual is the same rite the cult uses. It reveals the killers because the house recognizes its own. Completing it gives the investigators the fifth letter — and gives the cult the final witness it needs. Every escape is also a completion.

## The crime

The victim is scenario-defined. Common threads:

- The victim funded, covered up, or profited from the 1926 expedition.
- The cult wanted them dead for a specific reason: money, silence, bloodline, betrayal, or a debt.
- The killer staged the body as a ritual tableau: a room, a weapon, a signature, and a red herring.
- The staging is the killer's confession, but only if the investigators can read it.

## The cult's goal

Complete the Ninth Rite before 7:00 AM:

1. Ten souls in the house.
2. A witness who chooses to stay.
3. The Hollow Word spoken at the altar.

The investigators' escape prevents the completion. Their ritual accelerates it. The cult is willing to let them think they are winning.

## The investigators' goal

1. Solve the four Case File tasks.
2. Perform the Revealing Ritual.
3. Learn the Hollow Word.
4. Stop the killers.
5. Escape before dawn.

They do not know the ritual feeds the cult. That is the story's final turn.

## Themes

- **Old money hides old crimes.** The lodge is beautiful because someone paid for the silence.
- **The house remembers.** Architecture is a witness; the midnight shift is the house correcting the story.
- **A ritual is a story people agree to tell.** The cult believes; the investigators doubt; the house does not care.
- **The detective and the cultist are the same shape.** Both solve the crime; one wants the truth, one wants the ending.
- **Survival is not justice.** Escaping does not undo the nine.

## What the player knows at the start

- They were invited to Blackwood Lodge.
- Ten years ago, nine guests vanished.
- The case was closed.
- The host is missing.
- One guest is dead.
- One of the investigators is a killer.

## What the player learns

- The lodge was built over something the cult calls the Hollow.
- The nine guests were a failed rite.
- The survivor founded the cult.
- The letter came from a defector.
- The ritual is a trap.
- The Hollow Word is the only way out.

## What the player never learns for certain

- Whether the Hollow is real, a story, or a shared delusion.
- Whether Vesper is helping or using them.
- Whether Elias planned to die.
- Whether the cult survives the night.

Ambiguity is intentional. The game is about the story the survivors tell.
