# Blackwood Lodge (Story)

> [!note] Two pages, two jobs
> This page is the **story** of the lodge. [[02 Story/Blackwood Lodge (Story)|Blackwood Lodge]] in the World section is the **map** page. Keep lore here and layout there.

## The house as a character

The lodge is not haunted in the ghost sense. It is **wrong** in the way a story can be wrong: rooms that do not match the blueprint, a chapel with no windows, a cellar larger than the foundation, a clock that stopped and started again.

The house remembers the 1936 rite. The midnight shift is the house correcting the story. The rooms move because the story is being retold.

| Year | Event |

## The wrong architecture

- **The chapel** has no exterior windows and does not appear on the 1898 blueprint.
- **The cellar** extends under the garden, past the foundation line.
- **The ballroom** is visible from the drive but not from the garden.
- **The third-floor corridor** has one more door than the floor plan allows.
- **The clock** in the Foyer stopped at 12:00 on the night of the 1936 rite. It runs again tonight.
- **The mirrors** in the Gallery show the room a half-second late.
- **The antler chandelier** has one more antler than any deer.

## The chapel

- The ritual site (proposed).
- A stone altar with a circular carving.
- Antlers above the altar.
- No windows; candles only.
- Two entrances: the main door and the servant stair.
- A hidden third entrance from the cellar (Ghost Blueprint).
- The air is warm even when the rest of the house is cold.

## The cellar

- Wine racks, coal bins, a boiler, and a locked door that is not on the blueprint.
- The door leads to the chapel's hidden stair.
- The vault cabinet is here (second pistol).
- The cult kept records here; some are still legible.
- The cellar is the quietest room in the house.

## The shifting

- At midnight, one wing reconfigures.
- The house does not teleport players; it moves rooms around them.
- Doors seal, stairs extend, corridors bend.
- The shift is not random to the story: it is the house "correcting" the 1936 layout.
- The Ghost Blueprint shows the original layout, which is why it helps predict the shift.

## The house's rules

1. The house never lies about a clue; it lies about the shape of the story.
2. The house remembers the nine.
3. The house wants the rite completed.
4. The house will let the investigators leave only if they speak the word.
5. The house does not care who wins.

## The house's voice

The house speaks through:

- The clock.
- The pipes.
- The rain.
- The portraits.
- The antlers.
- The bell.
- A voice in the static that is never quite a word.

## What the house is not

- Not a ghost.
- Not a god.
- Not alive in a literal sense.
- Not on anyone's side.
