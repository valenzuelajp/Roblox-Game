# The Hollow Court

> [!spoiler] Spoiler warning
> The cult's doctrine and plan are revealed across the story. Do not link this page from the player wiki.

| Meeting place | The chapel under Blackwood Lodge |

| Goal | Complete the Ninth Rite and open the Hollow |
| Method | Invite, isolate, record, sacrifice |

## Creed

> *Nine voices to wake it. One witness to keep it. One word to open it.*

The Court believes that stories are alive, that a place can remember, and that a ritual is a story told with bodies. They do not worship a god. They worship a **listener** — the thing under the hill that hears the story and answers.

The cult is fictional. Do not map it onto a real religion or culture.

## Structure

| Rank | Role |
|---|---|
| **The Host** | Elias. Owns the lodge, chooses the guests, speaks the word. |
| **The Hand** | The player-killer. Stages the crime, removes obstacles, protects the rite. |
| **The Choir** | The staff and townspeople who know a little and do as they are told. |
| **The Guests** | The sacrifices. They do not know they are guests. |
| **The Witness** | The one who survives to tell the story. Elias was the witness in 1936. |

## The Ninth Rite

The rite has four stages:

1. **The Invitation.** The guests are brought to the lodge.
2. **The Tableau.** A crime is staged to make the guests tell the story the Court needs.
3. **The Reveal.** The witness names the killer; the house recognizes its own.
4. **The Word.** The Hollow Word is spoken at the altar.

The Court believes the rite needs:

- Nine souls.
- One witness who chooses to stay.
- One word spoken freely.

The 1936 rite failed because the witness ran. Elias has spent ten years learning how to make a witness stay.

## The 1936 failure

- Nine guests were invited.
- Eight died.
- Elias survived.
- The word was never spoken.
- The chapel was sealed.
- The town closed the case.

Elias's conclusion: the rite needed **ten** souls, not nine. The tenth is the witness who chooses to stay. The 1947 investigators are the ten.

## The 1947 plan

1. Invite the original detectives back.
2. Stage a crime that mirrors 1936.
3. Let them solve the Case File.
4. Let them perform the Revealing Ritual — the same rite, reversed.
5. Take the Hollow Word from them.
6. Complete the Ninth Rite at dawn.

The cult's plan depends on the investigators winning. That is the trap.

## Methods

- **Isolation:** cut the road, kill the phone, seed the storm.
- **Mirroring:** stage the crime like the old case.
- **Pressure:** mark the investigators, one at a time.
- **Misdirection:** plant one false clue, one false letter, one false witness.
- **Patience:** let the detectives do the work.

## Iconography

- Antlers: chandelier, coat rack, altar, bookends, the sigil.
- Open circle: the Hollow, the witness's eye, the ritual ring.
- Black wax: seals, letters, the victim's eyes.
- Bone white: candles, masks, the chapel walls.
- Tarnished gold: the word, the altar, Vesper's rings.

## What the cult is not

- Not a political movement.
- Not a real religion.
- Not a metaphor for a real group.
- Not omnipotent. It can be stopped, detained, exposed, and escaped.
- Not always right. The Hollow may be a story the cult tells itself.
