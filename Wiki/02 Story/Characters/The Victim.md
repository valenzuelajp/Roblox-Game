# The Victim

The victim is **not a fixed character**. Each scenario card defines a different VIP, a different reason the cult wanted them dead, and a different staged scene. The victim is the first clue and the emotional anchor of the night.

## Victim profile fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Name | The victim's public identity |
| Age / look | Costume, portrait, body props |
| Profession | Why they matter to the cult |
| Connection to 1926 | The old case |
| Cult motive | Money, silence, bloodline, betrayal, debt |
| Secret | What they knew or did |
| Last known action | What they were doing before death |
| Relationship to Elias | Guest, creditor, rival, accomplice |
| Relationship to the investigators | Why the team cares |

## Body staging

The killer stages the body during the shop. The staging must answer the four Case File tasks:

1. **Victim:** who they are.
2. **Method:** how they died.
3. **Scene:** where and how they were staged.
4. **Motive:** why the cult wanted them dead.

The body should be readable in 10 seconds and reward a closer look:

- Pose: seated, kneeling, laid out, displayed.
- Wound: clean, ritual, staged, hidden.
- Props: antlers, candles, black wax, a specific object.
- Missing item: a ring, a ledger, a photograph.
- Signature: the killer's tell.

## The victim as a clue

| Clue | What it reveals |
|---|---|
| Clothing | Profession, wealth, where they came from |
| Hands | Work, rings, ink, burns |
| Wound | Weapon type, angle, time |
| Pocket contents | Motive, connection, red herring |
| Pose | Ritual role, staging, signature |
| Missing object | What the cult wanted |
| Portrait / photo | Who they were to Elias |

## Rules

- The victim is always a VIP with a specific cult motive.
- The victim is always connected to the 1936 case.
- The victim is never a player.
- The victim's body is never moved after staging.
- The victim's identity is a Case File answer; the free clue gives a partial name, the item clue gives the full identity.
- The victim's outfit must match the scenario card.
- The victim must be readable on mobile.

## The emotional beat

The victim is not a prop. The first player to find the body should feel:

- **Recognition:** "I know this person."
- **Dread:** "This is the old case again."
- **Urgency:** "We have until dawn."
