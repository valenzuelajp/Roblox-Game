# Elias Blackwood

> [!spoiler] Spoiler warning
> Elias is the survivor and the cult leader. Do not use this page in the player wiki.

## At a glance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Role | Host of Blackwood Lodge; the Survivor; leader of the Hollow Court |
| Age | 60s |
| Look | Tall, thin, immaculate; a black suit with an antler pin; one glove; eyes that are always slightly wet |
| Voice | Quiet, warm, reasonable; never raises it |
| Secret | He survived the 1936 rite and has been rebuilding it for ten years |
| Status | NPC; not the player killer; not a vote target |
| Fate | Vanishes at arrival; appears at the ritual; can be confronted |

## Appearance

Elias looks like a banker who has been to a funeral and enjoyed it. His suit is old but perfect. His antler pin is small enough to miss. His left hand is always gloved. He smells of cedar and candle smoke.

## Voice

- Speaks softly; people lean in.
- Uses the investigators' names too often.
- Apologizes for the storm, the road, the food, the body.
- Never lies outright; he omits.
- When he is angry, he becomes more polite.

## History

- Born 1888, the last Blackwood heir.
- Inherited the lodge in 1926.
- Funded the expedition into the Hollow.
- Built the chapel over the site.
- Invited the nine guests in 1936.
- Survived because he ran.
- Founded the Hollow Court in the years after.
- Sent for the original detectives in 1947.

## The plan

1. Greet the investigators.
2. Let them find the body.
3. Let them solve the Case File.
4. Let them perform the ritual.
5. Take the word.
6. Complete the Ninth Rite at dawn.

He believes the investigators are not victims. They are **collaborators** — the story needs people who choose to tell it.

## What he believes

- The Hollow is real.
- The nine are not dead; they are remembered.
- A story told with enough conviction becomes true.
- The witness must choose to stay.
- He is not a murderer; he is an editor.

## What he fears

- Being forgotten.
- A witness who refuses to tell the story.
- The word spoken by someone who does not believe it.
- Vesper.

## Dialogue

### Arrival

- "Welcome to Blackwood Lodge. I am sorry about the road."
- "The house is older than it looks. So am I."
- "Dinner is at eight. The clocks are unreliable."
- "You closed the case once. I hope you will close it properly tonight."

### If questioned

- "The nine guests left. I have the papers to prove it."
- "I do not believe in ghosts, detective. I believe in architecture."
- "The chapel? A family chapel. Every old house has one."
- "You are asking the wrong questions. That is not a criticism; it is an invitation."

### At the ritual

- "You have done well. Better than the last ones."
- "Do not stop now. The word is almost yours."
- "Nine voices. One witness. One word. You are the witness."
- "I am not the killer. I am the reason there is a killer."

### If confronted

- "You cannot arrest a story."
- "The house will remember you. That is more than the town did."
- "I am sorry. That is not a lie."

## Gameplay role

- NPC host.
- Appears at arrival, midnight (briefly), and the ritual.
- Cannot be marked, killed, or voted.
- Can be questioned for lore.
- May reveal a true clue if the investigators earn it (proposed).
- His disappearance at arrival is a story beat, not a bug.

## Story arc

| Beat | What happens |
|---|---|
| Arrival | Greets the team; disappears before the body is found |
| Investigation | His study, letters, and portrait contain clues |
| Midnight | Seen at the chapel door; does not speak |
| Ritual | Watches from the altar; offers the team the word |
| Dawn | If the rite completes, he is young again (or appears to be); if it fails, he ages in seconds |
| Results | One line: "The house will remember you." |

## The ambiguity

Elias may be:

- A true believer.
- A con man who has convinced himself.
- A grieving survivor trying to undo 1936.
- A monster who needs the story more than the people.

Do not resolve it. The game is about the story the survivors tell.
