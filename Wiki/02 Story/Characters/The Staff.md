# The Staff

The staff are the house's memory. They have worked here longer than the investigators have been alive. They know the lodge is wrong; they have decided not to talk about it.

## The five

| Name | Role | Age | Knows | Secret |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mr. Finch** | Butler | 60s | Every room; every guest | He was the Choir's first recruit |
| **Ada** | Maid | 20s | What was cleaned after 1936 | She found the empty coffin |
| **Cook** | Cook | 50s | The cellar; the chapel stair | She feeds the thing under the hill (she thinks) |
| **Silas** | Groundskeeper | 40s | The woods; the road | He saw Elias walk out at dawn |
| **Rook** | Chauffeur | 30s | The cars; the road | He is not old enough to remember, but his father was |

## What they provide

- **Testimony:** a sentence that points to a task, a room, or a lie.
- **Keys:** one locked door per staff member (proposed).
- **Atmosphere:** the house feels lived-in and afraid.
- **Red herrings:** one staff member lies for a personal reason, not because they are the killer.
- **Lore:** the 1936 weekend, the nine guests, Elias's return.

## Testimony examples

### Mr. Finch
- "The clock stopped at twelve. We were told not to fix it."
- "The chapel has no windows. I have never asked why."
- "The master does not eat with the guests. He watches."

### Ada
- "I cleaned the ballroom for a week. The carpet was replaced, not washed."
- "There were nine coffins. One was empty."
- "The mirrors are covered at night. I do not uncover them."

### Cook
- "The cellar is bigger than the house. I do not go past the third rack."
- "The chapel stair is behind the boiler. The master uses it."
- "Something in the cellar breathes when the rain stops."

### Silas
- "The road washes out every October. The master plans for it."
- "I saw him walk out of the woods at dawn. He was not alone."
- "The pines lean toward the house. I do not know why."

### Rook
- "My father drove the nine guests. He never spoke after."
- "The cars are kept full. The master expects to leave."
- "If you need to get out, the old logging road is west of the gate."

## Gameplay role

- NPCs, not players.
- Cannot be marked, killed, or voted.
- Can be questioned.
- One staff member can be bribed with Shillings (proposed).
- One staff member can be intimidated (proposed).
- Their testimony is supporting evidence, not a definitive Case File answer.
- They never reveal the killer directly.

## The staff's fear

The staff are not brave. They are survivors. They will help the investigators if:

- The investigators are polite.
- The investigators promise to take them along.
- The investigators find proof that Elias is lying.
- The house is close to dawn.

They will not help if:

- They are threatened.
- The investigators accuse them.
- The killer is in the room.
- Elias has spoken to them recently.

## The Choir

Some staff are members of the Hollow Court's **Choir** — people who know a little and do as they are told. They are not the player killer. They are the cult's eyes and hands.

- Mr. Finch is the most loyal.
- Cook is the most afraid.
- Ada is the most likely to defect.
- Silas is the most likely to run.
- Rook is the most likely to help.
