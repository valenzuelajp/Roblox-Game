# Madame Vesper

> [!spoiler] Spoiler warning
> Vesper's history and motives are revealed across the story.

## At a glance

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Role | Curio Shop keeper; fortune teller; cult defector |
| Age | 50s–60s |
| Look | Layered shawls, rings on every finger, a black veil, silver hair, a smile that arrives before the joke |
| Voice | Low, amused, precise; a European accent that is never identified |
| Shop | A warm, cluttered room that smells of wax and dust |
| Secret | She sent the letter; she wants the cult stopped — or taken |
| Status | Not a killer; not a vote target; may appear in the lodge |

## Appearance

Vesper dresses like a woman who has been rich, lost it, and kept the jewelry. Her rings are real. Her shawl is patched. Her veil is black lace. She keeps a deck of tarot cards that is missing one card: the Tower.

## Voice

- Speaks in complete sentences.
- Never answers a question directly.
- Uses "dear" as a warning, not an endearment.
- Knows more than she says.
- When she is afraid, she stops performing.

## History

- Born in a port city; came to Ashcombe in 1924.
- Read cards for the Blackwood household.
- Joined the Hollow Court as its archivist.
- Helped Elias plan the 1936 rite.
- Survived because she was not invited.
- Defected after the nine died.
- Sent the letter in 1946.
- Runs the Curio Shop as a cover and a penance.

## What she wants

- To stop Elias.
- To keep the Hollow closed.
- To be forgiven, or to be important again.
- To survive the night.

She has not decided which she wants most. That ambiguity is the character.

## What she knows

- The nine names.
- The chapel's hidden stair.
- The Hollow Word's purpose.
- The 1936 failure.
- Elias's plan for the tenth guest.

## What she will not say

- Where Elias is.
- What the ritual really does.
- Why she sent the letter now.
- What she wants in return.

## Dialogue

### Shop

- "Welcome, detective. The house has been expecting you."
- "Buy what you need. The house charges interest."
- "The glass sees more than the eye. The eye sees more than the mouth."
- "You have one hundred shillings. Spend them like you have one night."

### Hints

- "The dead are not the only ones who lie."
- "A clue is a story someone wanted you to find. Ask who wanted it."
- "The clock stopped at twelve. It did not stop for you."
- "If the chapel is warm, do not stay."

### If pressed

- "I sent the letter because I am tired of funerals."
- "Elias believes the house needs ten. I believe the house needs to be left alone."
- "Do not trust the host. I wrote that for a reason."

### At the end

- "You survived. That is not the same as winning."
- "The word is not a key. It is a name. Be careful whose name you say."

## Gameplay role

- Runs the [[Curio Shop]] during the shop phase.
- Provides the opening letter and the first hint.
- May appear in the lodge at midnight as a neutral NPC (proposed).
- Can be questioned for lore, not for a definitive case answer.
- Cannot be marked, killed, or voted.

## Story arc

| Beat | What happens |
|---|---|
| Shop | Warm, theatrical, helpful |
| Arrival | Absent; a note on the counter |
| Midnight | Appears in the chapel doorway; warns the team |
| Ritual | Tries to stop the ritual or help it, depending on the team's choices |
| Dawn | Disappears before the results, leaving one ring on the altar |
