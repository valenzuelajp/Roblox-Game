# The Nine Guests

> [!spoiler] Spoiler warning
> This is the cold case at the heart of the game.

| # | Guest | Profession | Why invited | Fate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Arthur Vale** | Industrialist | Funded the 1926 expedition | Died at the table |
| 2 | **Senator Edith Harrow** | Politician | Buried the expedition report | Died in the ballroom |

## The tenth guest

The cult needed ten. The nine guests were the first nine. The tenth was supposed to be **the witness** — the one who would choose to stay and tell the story.

Elias was the witness. He ran. The rite failed.

## The cover-up

- The sheriff found Elias on the road at dawn.
- Elias said the guests had left in the night.
- The town searched the woods for a week.
- The case was closed as a disappearance.
- The funerals were closed-casket.
- The Blackwood family paid for the church roof and the sheriff's re-election.
- The nine names were never printed together again.

## The bodies

- Eight bodies were recovered.
- The ninth coffin was empty.
- The official story: the ninth guest ran away.
- The truth: the ninth guest was the witness, and the witness was Elias.

## What the investigators remember

Each investigator remembers a different part of the case:

- The report that was too clean.
- The photographs that were developed twice.
- The interview with Elias that lasted four hours and produced one paragraph.
- The night they decided to stop asking.

## The 1947 mirror

The killer's staging is a mirror of 1936:

- A guest at the table.
- A weapon from the expedition.
- A room that matches the old death.
- A signature that only the killer and the house understand.

The investigators are not solving a new crime. They are solving the old one again.
