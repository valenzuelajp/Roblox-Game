# Timeline

> [!note] Provisional
> Dates and events are proposed until the story round is completed.

## Before the game

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1802 | Ashcombe is founded around a timber mill. |
| 1898 | Blackwood Lodge is built by the Blackwood family. |
| 1926 | Elias Blackwood inherits the lodge. He funds an expedition into the Hollow. The chapel is built over the site. |
| 1927 | The mill flood ruins the town; the Blackwood family buys the ridge. |
| 1936 | Nine guests are invited for a weekend. The Ninth Rite fails. Eight die; Elias survives. The case is closed. |
| 1936–1946 | Elias rebuilds the cult as the Hollow Court. The lodge is abandoned. The town forgets. |
| 1946 | Madame Vesper sends a letter to the original detectives. |
| 1947 | The investigators arrive. The storm cuts the road. The game begins. |

## The 1936 weekend

| Day | Event |
|---|---|
| Friday | Nine guests arrive: a banker, a judge, a doctor, an actress, a journalist, a priest, a professor, an heiress, and a politician. |
| Saturday | The guests are shown the chapel. The staff are dismissed. The doors are locked at midnight. |
| Sunday | The rite begins. Eight guests die. Elias survives. The lodge burns part of the chapel. |
| Monday | The sheriff finds Elias on the road. The case is closed in a week. |
| The funeral | Nine coffins? Eight bodies and one empty coffin. The town does not ask. |

## The original investigators

The four player-investigators are the detectives who handled the 1936 case:

- The one who signed the report.
- The one who took the photographs.
- The one who interviewed Elias.
- The one who left the force.

They are not the same people as the guests. They are the people who let the case close.

## The deeper history

- The Hollow was known to the indigenous people of the valley as a place where stories go to be believed. (Proposed; handle with respect, avoid appropriation.)
- The Blackwood family built the lodge to own the story.
- The 1926 expedition found a chamber under the hill: a circular room with antlers, a stone table, and a word carved into the floor.
- The word is the Hollow Word; each scenario card has a different word because the house tells a different story each time.
- The cult believes the word is the name of the thing under the hill.
- The investigators believe the word is a passphrase.
- Both are right.
