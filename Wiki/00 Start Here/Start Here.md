# Start Here

Welcome to the design vault for **Blackwood Lodge** — a noir social-deduction game for Roblox.

## The pitch in one paragraph

Six to ten players arrive at a storm-cut mansion to solve a decade-old cult crime. One or two of them are the killers. There are no meeting phases and no safe table talk: the investigators hunt physical clues, spend their Shillings on the right tools, answer a four-question Case File, and perform a ritual that reveals the killers to everyone. The killers then get one last hunt. The survivors must reach the exit before dawn — or detain the killers for the full reward.

## What is already locked

- 6–10 players; 1 killer at 6–7, 2 partnered killers at 8–10.
- One continuous 30-minute match, 6 PM to 7 AM in-game, no rounds.
- A pre-match solo Curio Shop with 100 Shillings; unspent Shillings become Cinders.
- The killer stages the crime scene during the shop window.
- A four-question Case File plus a 30-second ritual. The old fifth question ("Who is the killer?") was removed.
- Free low-quality clues for every task; the matching investigator item reveals the definitive clue.
- Five puzzle types: zodiac, binary, blood cipher, rune alignment, knot/pattern.
- The sigil kill system: a visible mark, a physical sigil, a countdown, and a puzzle to break it.
- The first pistol at 1/4 solved tasks; the second pistol behind three seals; four bullets per pistol.
- Investigators have 1 HP; killers have 2 HP; each bullet deals 1 damage.
- The ritual reveals all killers to everyone and triggers the Last Hunt.
- The mansion layout, room placement, and clue placement are randomized every session.
- A five-letter Hollow Word unlocks the exit.

## What is still open

The full list lives in [[Open Questions]]. The biggest blockers:

1. What happens when an investigator dies? (spectator rules)
2. Is the first pistol random or voted?
3. Is there a pistol vote, a fate vote, or both?
4. Do investigators need to stop the killers *and* escape, or is escape enough?
5. How does detain/rescue work?
6. Is the ritual site the chapel?
7. What are the shop prices and the item catalog?
