## The victim

**Nora Finch** — Maid.

Twenties, mended uniform, a maid's cap, a list in her pocket, a sister in the house. She came back to give the dead their names.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** She saw the 1936 rite and kept a list of the nine guests.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Nora Finch was killed in **The Servant Quarters** with **Coal shovel**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **Nine buttons arranged in a circle**. The cult's reason was **Silence**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A stolen silver spoon in her pocket**.

The body is on the narrow servant bed, the coal shovel beside her, nine buttons arranged in a circle on the blanket. The silver spoon is in her pocket.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Servant Quarters

- **Signature:** Nine buttons arranged in a circle
- **Red herring:** A stolen silver spoon in her pocket
