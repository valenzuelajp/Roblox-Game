## The victim

**Father Anselm** — Priest.

Seventies, worn cassock, a rosary with a bent cross, a faith that has been in the house longer than the cult. He came to perform a blessing; he did not leave.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** He tried to bless the chapel and break the cult's hold on the house.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Father Anselm was killed in **The Chapel** with **Candlestick**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **An inverted candle on the altar**. The cult's reason was **Faith**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A torn page from an exorcism manual**.

The body is at the altar, the candlestick beside it, the candle inverted and still burning. The exorcism page is torn and placed in the hymnal.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Chapel
- **Body:** The body is at the altar, the candlestick beside it, the candle inverted and still burning. The exorcism page is torn and placed in the hymnal.
- **Weapon:** Candlestick
- **Signature:** An inverted candle on the altar
- **Red herring:** A torn page from an exorcism manual
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
