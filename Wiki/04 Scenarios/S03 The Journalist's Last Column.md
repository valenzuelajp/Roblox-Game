## The victim

**Julian Rook** — Investigative journalist.

Thirties, wet coat, a press card in his hat, a brother who drives the family car. He came to print the truth, not to survive it.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** He found the 1936 guest list and the tenth name.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Julian Rook was killed in **The Archive** with **Typewriter ribbon**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **Page nine circled in red ink**. The cult's reason was **Silence**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A suicide note typed on the victim's machine**.

The body is slumped over the archive desk, a typewriter ribbon around the neck, page nine of the 1936 file circled in red. The suicide note is still in the roller.

## The Hollow Word

The four task answers yield four letters. The ritual yields the fifth.

| Letter | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Task 1 — Victim | `T` |
| 2 | Task 2 — Method | `H` |
| 3 | Task 3 — Scene | `O` |
| 4 | Task 4 — Motive | `R` |
| 5 | Ritual | `N` |

**Word:** `THORN`

The gate lock accepts the word in any case. A wrong word locks the gate for 30 seconds and rings the chapel bell once; the killer hears it.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Archive
- **Body:** The body is slumped over the archive desk, a typewriter ribbon around the neck, page nine of the 1936 file circled in red. The suicide note is still in the roller.
- **Weapon:** Typewriter ribbon
- **Signature:** Page nine circled in red ink
- **Red herring:** A suicide note typed on the victim's machine
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
