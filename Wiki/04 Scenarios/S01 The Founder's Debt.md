## The victim

**Arthur Vale** — Industrialist.

Sixties, expensive coat, a ledger in every room, a man who bought silence by the acre. He is not a guest; he is a creditor.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** He funded the 1926 expedition and knew what the cult buried under the lodge.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Arthur Vale was killed in **The Study** with **Ice pick**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **A silver coin placed on each eye**. The cult's reason was **Silence**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A forged will leaving the estate to the Hollow Court**.

The body is seated in the study chair, facing the fire, with the coin on each eye and the ice pick wiped clean on the blotter. The killer leaves the letter opener on the rug as the obvious weapon.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Study
- **Body:** The body is seated in the study chair, facing the fire, with the coin on each eye and the ice pick wiped clean on the blotter. The killer leaves the letter opener on the rug as the obvious weapon.
- **Weapon:** Ice pick
- **Signature:** A silver coin placed on each eye
- **Red herring:** A forged will leaving the estate to the Hollow Court
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
