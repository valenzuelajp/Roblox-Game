## The victim

**Celia Blackwood** — Heiress.

Twenties, silver hairbrush, a deed in her coat, a niece's smile that never reaches her eyes. She is family, and family is the point.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** She was going to sell the lodge and give the land back to the town.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Celia Blackwood was killed in **The Master Suite** with **Laudanum**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **An antler hairpin placed on the pillow**. The cult's reason was **Inheritance**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A forged suicide note about debts**.

The body is in the master bed, the covers smooth, the antler hairpin on the pillow. The suicide note is on the nightstand, typed, not handwritten.

## The Hollow Word

The four task answers yield four letters. The ritual yields the fifth.

| Letter | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Task 1 — Victim | `C` |
| 2 | Task 2 — Method | `R` |
| 3 | Task 3 — Scene | `O` |
| 4 | Task 4 — Motive | `W` |
| 5 | Ritual | `N` |

**Word:** `CROWN`

The gate lock accepts the word in any case. A wrong word locks the gate for 30 seconds and rings the chapel bell once; the killer hears it.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Master Suite
- **Body:** The body is in the master bed, the covers smooth, the antler hairpin on the pillow. The suicide note is on the nightstand, typed, not handwritten.
- **Weapon:** Laudanum
- **Signature:** An antler hairpin placed on the pillow
- **Red herring:** A forged suicide note about debts
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
