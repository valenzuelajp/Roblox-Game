## The victim

**Lena Voss** — Pianist.

Thirties, silver gown, a cigarette in the piano keys, a song she will not finish. She came back to play the encore she never gave.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** She played at the 1936 rite and saw the guests go down to the chapel.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Lena Voss was killed in **The Ballroom** with **Piano wire**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **One piano key taped down**. The cult's reason was **Silence**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A smashed record of a love song**.

The body is behind the piano, the wire around the neck, one key taped down so the piano cannot play the final note. The record is smashed on the floor.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Ballroom
- **Body:** The body is behind the piano, the wire around the neck, one key taped down so the piano cannot play the final note. The record is smashed on the floor.
- **Weapon:** Piano wire
- **Signature:** One piano key taped down
- **Red herring:** A smashed record of a love song
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
