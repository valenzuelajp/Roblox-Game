## The victim

**Marguerite Hale** — Senator.

Fifties, red coat, a cigarette holder, a voice used to rooms that agree with her. She came to reopen a file, not to attend a party.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** She chaired the committee that buried the 1936 investigation.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Marguerite Hale was killed in **The Chapel** with **Bell cord**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **A bell rope tied in a hangman's knot**. The cult's reason was **Political exposure**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A letter from a rival senator threatening her**.

The body is laid across the altar steps, the bell rope around the neck, the chapel bell cracked once. The rival senator's letter is placed in her coat pocket.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Chapel
- **Body:** The body is laid across the altar steps, the bell rope around the neck, the chapel bell cracked once. The rival senator's letter is placed in her coat pocket.
- **Weapon:** Bell cord
- **Signature:** A bell rope tied in a hangman's knot
- **Red herring:** A letter from a rival senator threatening her
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
