## The victim

**Henry Mercer** — Architect.

Fifties, drafting tube, ink on his cuffs, a man who built a house he could not explain. He came back to correct the plans.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** He designed the shifting rooms and the hidden passages for the cult.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Henry Mercer was killed in **The Archive** with **Drafting compass**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **A blueprint with the number 10 circled**. The cult's reason was **Silence**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A set of keys to the front gate**.

The body is over the archive table, the drafting compass in the chest, the blueprint marked '10' circled. The gate keys are in his hand.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Archive
- **Body:** The body is over the archive table, the drafting compass in the chest, the blueprint marked '10' circled. The gate keys are in his hand.
- **Weapon:** Drafting compass
- **Signature:** A blueprint with the number 10 circled
- **Red herring:** A set of keys to the front gate
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
