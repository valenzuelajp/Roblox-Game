## The victim

**Thomas Rook** — Former chauffeur.

Sixties, uniform pressed, a cap with the initials 'T.R.', a son who took his job. He came back to see the old car; he did not leave.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** He drove the nine guests to the lodge in 1936 and knew where the bodies were buried.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Thomas Rook was killed in **The Carriage House** with **Carbon monoxide**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **Headlights left on**. The cult's reason was **Silence**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A bottle of whiskey and a suicide note**.

The body is in the driver's seat of the old car, the engine running, the carriage house doors closed, the headlights on. The whiskey and note are on the passenger seat.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Carriage House
- **Body:** The body is in the driver's seat of the old car, the engine running, the carriage house doors closed, the headlights on. The whiskey and note are on the passenger seat.
- **Weapon:** Carbon monoxide
- **Signature:** Headlights left on
- **Red herring:** A bottle of whiskey and a suicide note
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
