## The victim

**Dr. Amos Crane** — Physician.

Sixties, black bag, clean hands, a bedside manner that has outlived its patients. He knows what nine bodies look like when the cause is a lie.

**Why the cult wanted them dead:** He signed the 1936 death certificates and lied about the cause of death.

## The truth

On the night of the match, Dr. Amos Crane was killed in **The Cellar** with **Surgical knife**. The killer staged the body with a deliberate signature: **Nine stitches on the left sleeve**. The cult's reason was **Silence**. The scene is built to point at the red herring: **A medical bag with a bottle of morphine**.

The body is propped against the wine rack, the surgical knife in the chest, nine stitches on the left sleeve. The morphine bottle is open on the floor.

## The Hollow Word

The four task answers yield four letters. The ritual yields the fifth.

The gate lock accepts the word in any case. A wrong word locks the gate for 30 seconds and rings the chapel bell once; the killer hears it.

## Staging notes

- **Room:** The Cellar
- **Body:** The body is propped against the wine rack, the surgical knife in the chest, nine stitches on the left sleeve. The morphine bottle is open on the floor.
- **Weapon:** Surgical knife
- **Signature:** Nine stitches on the left sleeve
- **Red herring:** A medical bag with a bottle of morphine
- **Killer route:** use the room's second exit or the nearest secret passage. The body must be reachable before the chapel and vault seal at midnight.
- **Validation:** the room must be valid for the player count, reachable from the foyer, and not the ritual site or the vault.
