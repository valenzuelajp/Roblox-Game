## The room

### Surface story

A stone tower with a cracked bronze bell, a wooden ladder, a rope, and a view of the whole estate. The wind is strong.

### Cult story

The bell was removed in 1936, but it is still here. The crack is in the shape of a five-letter word. The rope is new.

### Deep story

The bell tower is the house's voice. The bell rings when the ritual begins and when the gate is wrong.

## What you see

- Cracked bronze bell
- Wooden ladder
- New rope
- Estate view
- Five-letter crack

## Clue role

Ritual bell; Task 3 Scene (bell rope); Task 4 Motive (1936 bell removal); map landmark.

## The secret

The bell can be rung to distract the killer or signal the team. The ladder leads to the roof and the Attic window.

## Killer advantage

The tower is a vertical chokepoint. The killer can cut the rope or camp the ladder.

## Investigator advantage

The bell is a team signal and a ritual anchor. The view reveals the map layout.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Wind, the cracked bell, a rope creaking.

**Light:** Storm light, a lantern, the bell's shadow.

## Midnight shift

The bell rings once. The crack widens. The rope hangs lower.

## Routes

Attic ladder, roof ledge, chapel bell rope (post-midnight), Observatory stairs.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
