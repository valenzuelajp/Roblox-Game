## The room

### Surface story

A brass bed, a rose-patterned wall, a suitcase, a washstand, and a window over the front gate. The room is tidy except for the bed.

### Cult story

The suitcase belongs to the victim. The rose wallpaper hides a water stain shaped like an antler. The mirror has a crack.

### Deep story

The guest rooms are where the visitors slept. The house remembers them in the wallpaper.

## What you see

- Brass bed
- Locked suitcase
- Cracked mirror
- Washstand
- Gate window

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (suitcase, monogram); Task 4 Motive (letter in the suitcase).

## The secret

The suitcase has a false lining with a 1936 ticket. The mirror opens a narrow passage to Guest Room Ivy.

## Killer advantage

The room is small and has one door. The killer can corner a searcher, but the mirror passage is an escape.

## Investigator advantage

The gate window is a lookout. The suitcase is a strong Task 1 clue.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** A window rattling, a suitcase lock, rain on the glass.

**Light:** A bedside lamp, gate light, lightning.

## Midnight shift

The suitcase opens. The bed is made. The rose wallpaper blooms.

## Routes

First-floor corridor, mirror passage to Guest Room Ivy, window to the gate roof (risky).

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
