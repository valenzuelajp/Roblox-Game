## The room

### Surface story

A low-beamed attic full of trunks, dust sheets, a dressmaker's mannequin, and a round window. The floor creaks.

### Cult story

The trunks hold the nine guests' belongings, each labeled with a name and a date. The mannequin wears a cult robe. The round window has an antler shadow.

### Deep story

The attic is where the house stores what it cannot explain. The 1926 furniture is arranged for a dinner that never ended.

## What you see

- Nine trunks
- Dressmaker's mannequin
- Cult robe
- Round window
- 1926 furniture

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (trunk labels); Task 4 Motive (guest belongings); Task 3 Scene (mannequin staging).

## The secret

The mannequin has a key to the Bell Tower in its pocket. The round window opens onto the roof and the Bell Tower ledge.

## Killer advantage

The attic is a maze of trunks. The killer can hide, and the creaking floor is an early warning.

## Investigator advantage

The nine trunks are the cold case in physical form. The roof route is an escape.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Creaking, wind in the eaves, a trunk lid.

**Light:** A bare bulb, a round window, dust in the beam.

## Midnight shift

The mannequin turns. The trunks open. The 1926 table is set for ten.

## Routes

Attic stairs, roof ledge to Bell Tower, service stairs to First Floor, chimney to Archive.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
