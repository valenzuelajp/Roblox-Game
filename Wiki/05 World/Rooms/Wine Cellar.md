## The room

### Surface story

Floor-to-ceiling racks, a tasting table, a broken chair, and a smell of cork and mold. The bottles are dusty except one.

### Cult story

The 1926 bottle is clean. It is not wine; it is a scroll case. The rack behind it is a false wall.

### Deep story

The wine cellar is a decoy: the cult does not drink. The real cellar is behind the rack.

## What you see

- 1926 bottle
- Tasting table
- False rack
- Broken chair
- Corkscrew

## Clue role

Task 4 Motive (1926 bottle, toast); Task 3 Scene (blood trail behind rack).

## The secret

The false rack opens a narrow passage to the Vault antechamber. It is the killer's favorite route.

## Killer advantage

The racks create narrow sightlines. The killer can ambush a searcher or hide a body in the dark.

## Investigator advantage

The 1926 bottle is a strong Task 4 clue. The passage is a shortcut to the Vault.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Bottles settling, a cork popping, water dripping.

**Light:** A caged bulb, candle stubs, total dark in the back.

## Midnight shift

The 1926 bottle turns to dust. The false rack opens wider. The tasting table is set for ten.

## Routes

The Cellar, false rack to Vault antechamber, narrow stairs to Dining Room (service).

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
