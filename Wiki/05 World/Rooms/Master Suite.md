## The room

### Surface story

A four-poster bed, a writing desk, a wardrobe, a fireplace, and a balcony door. The room is cold and smells of cedar.

### Cult story

The wardrobe has a hidden compartment with a cult robe and a letter from Vesper. The writing desk has a draft of the 1946 letter.

### Deep story

The master suite is Elias's attempt at a normal life. The robe is the only thing that fits.

## What you see

- Four-poster bed
- Writing desk
- Wardrobe
- Cult robe
- Balcony door

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (letter, photograph); Task 4 Motive (Vesper letter, robe); Task 3 Scene (staged bed).

## The secret

The wardrobe backs onto a servant passage to the Nursery. The balcony has a rope ladder to the Conservatory roof.

## Killer advantage

The suite is private and locked. A body here is found late. The balcony is a quick escape.

## Investigator advantage

The Vesper letter is a major story clue. The servant passage is a shortcut.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** A clock, wind in the chimney, a rope tapping the balcony.

**Light:** Firelight, a bedside lamp, cold moonlight.

## Midnight shift

The bed is turned down. The robe is on the chair. The letter is signed in the victim's hand.

## Routes

First-floor corridor, servant passage to Nursery, balcony rope to Conservatory roof, service stairs.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
