## The room

### Surface story

A narrow spiral stair, wet stone, iron rings in the wall, and a door at the top with no handle. The air is colder than the cellar.

### Cult story

The rings are for the guests who did not walk. The door has an antler carved on the chapel side. The stair is older than the house.

### Deep story

The crypt stair is the original entrance to the Hollow. The chapel was built to cover it.

## What you see

- Iron rings
- Stone door
- Candle niches
- Scratches on the wall
- Cold air

## Clue role

Ritual route; Task 3 Scene (drag marks); Ghost Blueprint lore.

## The secret

The stair has a side niche with a 1926 coin. The door opens only after midnight or with the Ghost Blueprint.

## Killer advantage

The stair is a chokepoint. The killer can wait at the bottom or block the top.

## Investigator advantage

The stair is the fastest ritual route. The niche is a hiding spot for a small item.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Water, footsteps, a bell above, breathing.

**Light:** Candle niches, total dark, a cold glow from the chapel door.

## Midnight shift

The door opens. The candles light. The scratches form a word.

## Routes

Cellar to Chapel, side niche, vault drain.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
