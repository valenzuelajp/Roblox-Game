## The room

### Surface story

A cast-iron boiler, a coal pile, pipes wrapped in cloth, and a fuse box with a brass handle. The room is hot and loud.

### Cult story

The boiler burns something that is not coal: bone-white ash and a sweet smell. The fuse box has a second switch labeled 'CHAPEL'.

### Deep story

The boiler is the house's heart. The chapel switch is the house's pulse.

## What you see

- Cast-iron boiler
- Coal pile
- Fuse box
- Pipe wrench
- Bone-white ash

## Clue role

Task 2 Method (pipe wrench, ash); Task 3 Scene (heat, ash); sabotage target (flicker).

## The secret

The 'CHAPEL' switch cuts the chapel lights and unlocks the crypt stair for 15 seconds. The boiler has a crawl space to the coal chute.

## Killer advantage

The boiler is loud enough to cover a struggle. The fuse box can black out a wing.

## Investigator advantage

The fuse box can restore lights or cut the killer's escape. The heat is a tell for the UV torch.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** The boiler roar, pipes knocking, coal shifting.

**Light:** Furnace red, a single bulb, sparks.

## Midnight shift

The boiler goes silent. The 'CHAPEL' switch flips itself. The ash forms a word.

## Routes

The Cellar, coal chute to Carriage House, service stairs to Kitchen.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
