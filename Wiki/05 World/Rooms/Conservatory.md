# Conservatory

> [!info] Room role

A glass room full of dead plants and living rain. The best place to see the storm and be seen.

## The room

### Surface story

A iron-and-glass roof, a tiled floor, potted ferns, a dry fountain, and a door to the garden. The rain is deafening.

### Cult story

The fountain is dry, but the basin is full of black water. A stone antler is buried in the soil of the largest fern.

### Deep story

The conservatory is the house's attempt at life. It is the first room the cult let die.

## What you see

- Dry fountain
- Black water
- Stone antler
- Potted ferns
- Garden door

## Clue role

Task 3 Scene (wet footprints, glass reflections); Task 4 Motive (antler, garden path).

## The secret

The fountain has a false bottom with a key to the Carriage House. The garden door leads to the woods path and the front gate.

## Killer advantage

The glass walls make the room a fishbowl. The killer can see approach from three directions and escape through the garden.

## Investigator advantage

The garden door is a potential escape route. The fountain is a hiding spot for small items.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Rain on glass, water dripping, a wind chime with no wind.

**Light:** Grey storm light, a single lantern, lightning through the glass.

## Midnight shift

The fountain fills with clear water. The plants bloom black. The garden path appears on the map.

## Routes

Foyer corridor, Study window, Ballroom French doors, Chapel side door, garden to Carriage House and front gate.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
