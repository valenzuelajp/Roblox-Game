## The room

### Surface story

Floor-to-ceiling shelves, a rolling ladder, a reading desk, and a globe with the Ashcombe woods marked in red. The air is dry and cold.

### Cult story

The expedition books are shelved under fiction. A hollow atlas holds a map of the chapel's original foundation. The ladder has a hidden latch.

### Deep story

The library is the house's attempt to organize the truth. It failed.

## What you see

- Rolling ladder
- Hollow atlas
- Expedition books
- Reading desk
- Globe

## Clue role

Task 4 Motive (expedition records); Task 1 Victim (guest list cross-reference); Ghost Blueprint lore.

## The secret

The ladder latches into a wall panel that opens a crawl route to the Study fireplace. The atlas has a hidden map pocket.

## Killer advantage

The shelves create blind corners. The killer can hide in the stacks and listen to the team's deductions.

## Investigator advantage

The library is the best place to find the 1926 story. The crawl route is a quick escape.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Pages turning, the ladder wheels, rain on the skylight.

**Light:** Green reading lamps, a skylight full of lightning.

## Midnight shift

The expedition books move from fiction to history. The globe's red mark moves to the lodge.

## Routes

Foyer west corridor, Study flue (crawl), Conservatory door, Archive stairs.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
