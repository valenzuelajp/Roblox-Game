## The room

### Surface story

A round stone room with a steel cabinet, three brass seals on the wall, and a drain in the floor. The air is cold and dry.

### Cult story

The seals are the three puzzle families: zodiac, binary, knot. The cabinet holds a pistol, four bullets, and a ledger of the cult's debts.

### Deep story

The vault is the cult's treasury. The pistol is the only thing in it that can kill a killer.

## What you see

- Steel cabinet
- Three brass seals
- Drain
- Debt ledger
- Empty pedestal

## Clue role

Second pistol; three seals; Task 4 Motive (debt ledger).

## The secret

The drain leads to the crypt stair. The cabinet has a false back with a fifth bullet? No — four bullets only.

## Killer advantage

The killer can camp the vault or claim the pistol. The single entrance is a death trap if the team corners them.

## Investigator advantage

The vault is the power spike. The three seals are a team puzzle; the cabinet is first-come.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** A low hum, seals clicking, water in the drain.

**Light:** Cold blue, brass reflections, a single shaft from the ceiling.

## Midnight shift

The seals glow. The cabinet unlocks. The drain fills with warm air.

## Routes

The Cellar antechamber, drain to crypt stair, secret passage from Wine Cellar.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
