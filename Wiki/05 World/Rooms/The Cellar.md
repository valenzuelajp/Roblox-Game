## The room

### Surface story

A low stone room with a coal scuttle, a wine rack, a boiler, and three doors. The ceiling is wet. The floor is newer than the walls.

### Cult story

The floor is newer because something was buried under it. The wine rack hides a sealed door with an antler carved into the stone.

### Deep story

The cellar is the house's foundation and its first secret. The crypt stair goes deeper than the plans allow.

## What you see

- Coal scuttle
- Wine rack
- Boiler
- Sealed stone door
- New floor

## Clue role

Task 2 Method (coal shovel, surgical kit); Task 3 Scene (blood trail, sigil); Vault access.

## The secret

The sealed door behind the wine rack opens with the Ghost Blueprint. It leads to the crypt stair and the vault antechamber.

## Killer advantage

The cellar is a maze of pillars and racks. The killer can lose a chase here. The coal chute is a second exit.

## Investigator advantage

The cellar connects the kitchen, the vault, and the crypt. The boiler can be used to make noise or light.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Dripping water, the boiler, coal shifting, a distant bell.

**Light:** A single bulb, boiler fire, a flashlight beam.

## Midnight shift

The sealed door opens. The boiler goes out. The floor is dry for the first time.

## Routes

Kitchen stairs, Boiler Room, Wine Cellar, Vault antechamber, crypt stair (post-midnight), coal chute to Carriage House.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
