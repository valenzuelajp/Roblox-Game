> [!info] Room role

## The room

### Surface story

A double staircase, a chandelier, a black-and-white marble floor, and a portrait of a man whose face has been cut out. The front doors are heavy oak; the storm presses against the glass.

### Cult story

The guest book has ten lines, not nine. The portrait's plaque is blank. A brass bell by the door rings once when the chapel opens.

### Deep story

The foyer is the house's mouth. Every route passes through it, and the house knows who leaves.

## What you see

- Guest book with ten lines
- Cut portrait
- Brass bell
- Umbrella stand with a silver-tipped cane
- Evidence board

## Clue role

Evidence board hub; Task 1 guest list cross-reference; no free clue is placed here.

## The secret

The portrait hinges open into a shallow cavity with a 1926 room key. The cavity is empty if the killer found it first.

## Killer advantage

The foyer is the best place to watch who arrives and who leaves. The killer can turn the evidence board once per match.

## Investigator advantage

The evidence board, the guest book, and the front door make the foyer the safest place to compare notes. It has three exits.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Rain on glass, the chandelier ticking, the front door breathing in the wind.

**Light:** Warm chandelier, cold lightning through the transom, a single candle on the board table.

## Midnight shift

The portrait changes: the victim is now in it. The guest book gains a tenth line in the victim's hand.

## Routes

Front gate (locked), grand staircase to First Floor, service corridor to Kitchen, east corridor to Ballroom, west corridor to Study, cellar door to Cellar.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
