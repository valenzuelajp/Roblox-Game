## The room

### Surface story

A stone balcony with a balustrade, two chairs, a dead plant, and a view of the front gate and the storm. The floor is wet.

### Cult story

The balustrade has ten carved roses; one is fresh. The dead plant is a black fern from the conservatory.

### Deep story

The balcony is the house's stage for watching arrivals. The house likes to see who comes.

## What you see

- Stone balustrade
- Two chairs
- Dead fern
- Wet floor
- Gate view

## Clue role

Task 3 Scene (wet footprints, balcony route); Task 1 Victim (gate arrival).

## The secret

A rope ladder is tied to the balustrade and hidden under the dead fern. It leads to the Conservatory roof.

## Killer advantage

The balcony is exposed but has a rope escape. The killer can watch the gate and drop into the conservatory.

## Investigator advantage

The gate view is a lookout. The rope ladder is a quick route to the Conservatory.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Wind, rain, the gate creaking, a rope tapping.

**Light:** Storm light, a single lantern, lightning.

## Midnight shift

The tenth rose blooms. The gate opens. The rope ladder is wet.

## Routes

First-floor corridor, Master Suite balcony door, rope ladder to Conservatory roof, drainpipe to Guest Room Ivy.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
