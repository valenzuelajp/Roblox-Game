> [!info] Room role

## The room

### Surface story

A parquet floor, a grand piano, a chandelier wrapped in dust sheets, and tall windows that look into the storm. The room smells of champagne and cold ash.

### Cult story

The 1936 program is still on the piano: nine performers, one blank line. The mirrored wall reflects ten chairs when there are only nine.

### Deep story

The ballroom was built for an audience. The house still expects one.

## What you see

- Grand piano with one taped key
- 1936 program
- Champagne glasses
- Mirrored wall
- Dust sheets

## Clue role

Task 3 Scene clues; the taped key; the 1936 program supports Task 1 or 4.

## The secret

The mirror is a false mirror: a service passage behind it leads to the Chapel crypt. It opens only after midnight.

## Killer advantage

The ballroom is public, loud, and full of shadows. The killer can stage a body here and be seen leaving by a dozen witnesses.

## Investigator advantage

The piano and the mirror give audio and visual tells. The room has four exits, so it is hard to trap.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** The piano sustains one note too long; the chandelier crystals click; the rain sounds like applause.

**Light:** Dusty chandelier, blue lightning, a single spotlight on the piano.

## Midnight shift

The mirror passage opens. The piano plays one note by itself. The program's blank line fills with the victim's name.

## Routes

Foyer east corridor, Kitchen service door, Conservatory French doors, mirrored passage to Chapel crypt (post-midnight).

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
