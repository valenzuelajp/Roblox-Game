## The room

### Surface story

A long table, a dusty chandelier, silver candlesticks, and a sideboard with a decanter. The chairs are pushed in except one.

### Cult story

The place cards have the nine guests' names, but the tenth is blank. The decanter is filled with water, not wine.

### Deep story

The dining room is a rehearsal for the ritual: ten seats, one empty, a host who never sits.

## What you see

- Place cards
- Silver candlesticks
- Decanter
- Sideboard
- Ten chairs

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (place cards, seating chart); Task 4 Motive (toast card, seating politics).

## The secret

The sideboard has a hidden drawer with a 1936 menu and a seating plan that puts the victim at the head.

## Killer advantage

The long table blocks sightlines. The killer can stage a body at one end and watch the other.

## Investigator advantage

The place cards are a quick Task 1 clue. The room has two doors and a pass-through to the Kitchen.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Silverware settling, a chair scraping, a clock ticking.

**Light:** Chandelier, candlelight, a cold window.

## Midnight shift

The tenth chair pulls out. The blank place card fills in. The decanter turns red.

## Routes

Foyer east corridor, Kitchen pass-through, Ballroom service door, Conservatory corridor.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
