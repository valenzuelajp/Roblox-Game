## The room

### Surface story

Metal shelves, file boxes, a map table, a typewriter, and a smell of dust and ink. The window is painted shut.

### Cult story

The 1936 file is complete except for the autopsy photo. The 1926 map is filed under 1936. The typewriter has a ribbon that matches the victim's wound.

### Deep story

The archive is the house's alibi. Every lie has a folder.

## What you see

- 1936 file
- 1926 map
- Typewriter
- Map table
- File boxes

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (guest list); Task 2 Method (typewriter ribbon); Task 4 Motive (expedition file); Ghost Blueprint.

## The secret

The map table has a hidden drawer with the Ghost Blueprint. The typewriter's carriage return opens a wall panel to the Library.

## Killer advantage

The archive has one door and a wall panel. The killer can trap a searcher or listen through the panel.

## Investigator advantage

The archive is the best place to solve the case. The wall panel is a quick escape to the Library.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** A typewriter, paper sliding, rain on the painted window.

**Light:** Green desk lamps, a cold window, a single bulb.

## Midnight shift

The typewriter types the victim's name. The 1936 file gains an autopsy photo. The map table turns.

## Routes

First-floor corridor, Library wall panel, service stairs to Kitchen, attic stairs.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
