## The room

### Surface story

Four narrow beds, a washbasin, a hanging rail of uniforms, a small stove, and a window over the back garden. The room is clean and cold.

### Cult story

The uniforms are labeled with the staff's names, but one bed has no name. The stove has a hidden letter from Nora Finch.

### Deep story

The servants' quarters are where the house's witnesses slept. The empty bed is the one the house keeps for the next maid.

## What you see

- Four narrow beds
- Uniform rail
- Small stove
- Nora's letter
- Empty bed

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (Nora's cap, photograph); Task 4 Motive (letter); Task 3 Scene (staged bed).

## The secret

The empty bed has a loose floorboard with a key to the Archive. The window has a servant stair to the Kitchen.

## Killer advantage

The room is a dead end with one door and a window. The killer can trap a searcher, but the servant stair is an escape.

## Investigator advantage

Nora's letter is an emotional clue. The servant stair is a quick route to the Kitchen.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** A kettle, a uniform hanger, a bedspring.

**Light:** A small stove, a cold window, a candle.

## Midnight shift

The empty bed is made. The uniform rail gains a tenth uniform. The letter is signed.

## Routes

Attic stairs, servant stair to Kitchen, window to back garden, loose floorboard to Archive key.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
