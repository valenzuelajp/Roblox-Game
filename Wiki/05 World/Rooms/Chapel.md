## The room

### Surface story

A small stone chapel with ten pews, nine hymnals, a stained-glass window of nine guests and one empty pane, and an altar with five candles. The bell rope hangs by the door.

### Cult story

The altar faces the wrong direction. The fifth candle is black. The crypt stair is behind the altar, sealed by a stone door.

### Deep story

The chapel was built over the Hollow. The house grew around it. The ritual is the house's oldest room.

## What you see

- Altar
- Five candles
- Nine hymnals
- Stained-glass window
- Cracked bell rope

## Clue role

Ritual site; Task 3 Scene (chapel staging); Task 4 Motive (priest's notes); fifth letter under the altar with UV.

## The secret

The crypt stair leads to the Cellar. The bell rope is a second route to the Bell Tower. The side door opens only from the inside after midnight.

## Killer advantage

The chapel is a chokepoint. The killer can mark the channeler or break an anchor. The crypt stair gives a second entrance.

## Investigator advantage

Two entrances after midnight, a bell rope, and the altar give the team a defensible ritual site.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Rain on stone, a bell that is not there, a hymn hummed by no one.

**Light:** Candles, stained-glass color, no shadows.

## Midnight shift

The chapel unseals. The fifth candle lights. The bell rings once. The stained-glass empty pane fills with the killer's silhouette.

## Routes

Foyer east corridor, side door to Conservatory, crypt stair to Cellar, bell rope to Bell Tower.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
