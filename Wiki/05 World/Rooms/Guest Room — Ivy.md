## The room

### Surface story

A narrow bed, ivy-patterned wallpaper, a wardrobe, a writing desk, and a window over the garden. The room smells of dust and perfume.

### Cult story

The wardrobe has a child's coat and a 1936 photograph. The ivy wallpaper hides a message in the pattern: 'TEN'.

### Deep story

The ivy room was the nursery before it was a guest room. The coat is the only thing that never aged.

## What you see

- Narrow bed

- Wardrobe
- Garden window

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (photograph); Task 3 Scene (child's coat, hidden message); Task 4 Motive (1936 photo).

## The secret

The wardrobe backs onto the mirror passage from Rose. The window has a drainpipe route to the Conservatory.

## Killer advantage

The room is quiet and out of the way. The killer can hide here and listen to the corridor.

## Investigator advantage

The photograph is a story clue. The drainpipe is an escape route.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Ivy scratching, a floorboard, a music box.

**Light:** A dim lamp, garden light, a candle.

## Midnight shift

The music box plays. The child's coat is gone. The ivy spells a name.

## Routes

First-floor corridor, mirror passage to Rose, drainpipe to Conservatory, servant passage to Master Suite.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
