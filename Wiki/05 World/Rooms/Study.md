## The room

### Surface story

A leather chair, a mahogany desk, a wall of law books, and a fireplace with a fire that has burned too long. The room smells of pipe smoke and old paper.

### Cult story

The desk drawer has a false bottom with a 1926 expedition map. The law books are hollow; one holds a ledger of the nine guests.

### Deep story

The study is where the lie was written. The house keeps the original.

## What you see

- Mahogany desk
- False-bottom drawer
- 1926 map
- Hollow law book
- Fireplace

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (guest list, portrait plaque); Task 2 Method (letter opener, blotter); Task 4 Motive (ledger, letters).

## The secret

The fireplace has a second flue that leads to the Library. It is a crawl route, not a walk route.

## Killer advantage

The study has two doors and a window. The killer can enter from the Library flue and leave by the corridor.

## Investigator advantage

The desk and the fire give light and cover. The room is a natural base for the evidence board.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** The fire, a clock that is ten minutes fast, a pen scratching when no one writes.

**Light:** Firelight, a green banker's lamp, lightning through the window.

## Midnight shift

The clock stops at 11:40. The false bottom is open. The 1926 map has a new room drawn on it.

## Routes

Foyer west corridor, Library flue (crawl), window to Conservatory, service corridor to Kitchen.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
