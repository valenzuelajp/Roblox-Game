## The room

### Surface story

A small bed, a rocking horse, a toy chest, a mural of the woods, and a window that will not open. The air is warm.

### Cult story

The mural has ten animals; one is a stag with a human face. The toy chest has a 1926 birth certificate with the name blank.

### Deep story

The nursery is the house's idea of a future. It is the room the cult could not complete.

## What you see

- Rocking horse
- Toy chest
- Woods mural
- Birth certificate
- Locked window

## Clue role

Task 1 Victim (birth certificate); Task 4 Motive (bloodline); Task 3 Scene (rocking horse).

## The secret

The toy chest has a false bottom with a key to the Observatory. The mural has a hidden door to the servant passage.

## Killer advantage

The nursery is small and warm; the killer can hide behind the mural door. The rocking horse is a sound trap.

## Investigator advantage

The birth certificate is a bloodline clue. The mural door is a shortcut to the Master Suite.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** A rocking horse, a music box, a child humming.

**Light:** A nightlight, a warm lamp, no shadows.

## Midnight shift

The rocking horse moves. The mural's stag turns to face the room. The window opens.

## Routes

First-floor corridor, mural door to servant passage, service stairs to Kitchen, Observatory key.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
