## The room

### Surface story

A brass telescope, a rotating dome, a star chart, a desk, and a case of lenses. The room is cold and smells of brass polish.

### Cult story

The star chart is not the sky; it is the lodge's floor plan. The telescope points at the chapel, not the stars. The lens case has a zodiac cipher.

### Deep story

The observatory is the cult's attempt to read the house as a constellation. The house is the sky.

## What you see

- Brass telescope
- Rotating dome
- Star chart
- Lens case
- Zodiac cipher

## Clue role

Zodiac puzzle; Task 4 Motive (star chart); Task 3 Scene (telescope view); map reveal.

## The secret

The telescope's lens projects the current map onto the dome when aligned with the chapel. The desk has a key to the Vault seals.

## Killer advantage

The dome is a echo chamber; the killer can hear the team below. The single stair is a trap.

## Investigator advantage

The telescope reveals the map and the zodiac puzzle. The dome is a lookout.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** The dome rotating, wind, a lens clicking.

**Light:** Starlight, a brass lamp, the telescope's reflection.

## Midnight shift

The dome opens. The star chart changes. The telescope points at the gate.

## Routes

Attic stairs, roof ledge, Bell Tower stairs, dome hatch (locked).

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
