## The room

### Surface story

A long worktable, a coal stove, copper pans, and a pantry that smells of bread and spice. Ten plates are stacked by the sink.

### Cult story

The pantry has a false back with a ledger of the 1936 menus. The tenth plate is chipped in the shape of an antler.

### Deep story

The kitchen is where the house feeds its guests. The cellar door is where it feeds something else.

## What you see

- Ten plates
- Coal stove
- Pantry ledger
- Butcher's block
- Cellar key on a hook

## Clue role

Task 2 Method clues (knives, cleaver); Task 4 Motive (ledger, menus); the cellar key is a route clue.

## The secret

The dumbwaiter runs from the pantry to the Cellar and the first-floor Linen Room. It is too small for a player but large enough for a clue.

## Killer advantage

The kitchen has one main door and a service door; the killer can block one and trap a searcher. The cellar key is a useful lure.

## Investigator advantage

The staff pass through here. The Cook's testimony is close, and the cellar route is fast.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** A kettle that will not whistle, the coal stove ticking, a knife on the block.

**Light:** Warm stove light, a hanging bulb, a cold rectangle of window.

## Midnight shift

The stove goes out. The ten plates are stacked in a different order. The cellar door unlocks.

## Routes

Foyer service corridor, Ballroom service door, Dining Room pass-through, Cellar stairs, dumbwaiter (clue only).

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
