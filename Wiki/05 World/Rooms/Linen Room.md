## The room

### Surface story

Floor-to-ceiling shelves of linen, a folding table, a laundry chute, and a dumbwaiter door. The room smells of soap and dust.

### Cult story

One sheet is stained with a sigil. The dumbwaiter has a note from the killer's predecessor. The laundry chute goes to the Cellar.

### Deep story

The linen room is the house's clean surface. The stain is the truth underneath.

## What you see

- Linen shelves
- Folding table
- Laundry chute
- Dumbwaiter
- Stained sheet

## Clue role

Task 3 Scene (stained sheet); Task 2 Method (laundry chute route); hiding spot for small items.

## The secret

The laundry chute is a one-way drop to the Cellar (safe landing, 1 HP? No damage). The dumbwaiter connects to the Kitchen pantry.

## Killer advantage

The room is small and quiet. The killer can hide behind the linen shelves. The chute is an escape.

## Investigator advantage

The chute is a fast route to the Cellar. The dumbwaiter is a clue route.

## Sound and light

**Sound:** Sheets shifting, the chute, a dumbwaiter bell.

**Light:** A single bulb, a high window, a candle.

## Midnight shift

The stained sheet is folded. The dumbwaiter opens. The chute smells of rain.

## Routes

Attic stairs, laundry chute to Cellar, dumbwaiter to Kitchen, service stairs.

## Design notes

- **Readability:** the room must be legible in blackout. Every critical clue has a text card and a lit fallback.
- **No soft-locks:** the room must have at least two routes or a key that is not behind its own lock.
- **No gore:** blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
- **Voice optional:** every interaction has a text prompt and a ping.
- **Performance:** keep unique props under 40; reuse modular props across rooms.
- **Accessibility:** no clue may depend on color alone; audio clues have subtitles.
