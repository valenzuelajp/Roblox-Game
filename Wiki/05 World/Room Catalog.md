# Room Catalog

> [!info] How to use this page
> The launch map draws **24–28 rooms** from this catalog. Anchors are always present; modular rooms are placed by the seed. A room page describes the fixed fiction and the gameplay role, not a fixed position.

## All rooms

|---|---|---|---|
| [[Foyer\|Foyer]] | Ground | Anchor | The first room the investigators see and the last room before the gate. The evidence board lives here. |
| [[Ballroom\|Ballroom]] | Ground | Anchor | A public stage for the killer and a sound trap for the investigators. The piano is always in tune. |
| [[Kitchen\|Kitchen]] | Ground | Anchor | The staff's territory and the quickest route to the cellar. The food is still warm. |
| [[Study\|Study]] | Ground | Anchor | The victim's room, the killer's stage, and the house's paperwork. The fire is always lit. |
| [[Chapel\|Chapel]] | Ground | Anchor / Ritual Site | The ritual site. Sealed until midnight. The only room in the house with no shadows. |
| [[Dining Room\|Dining Room]] | Ground | Modular | A social room that hides the guest list in plain sight. Ten chairs, nine place settings. |
| [[Library\|Library]] | Ground | Modular | The house's memory. The 1926 expedition is filed under fiction. |
| [[Conservatory\|Conservatory]] | Ground | Modular | A glass room full of dead plants and living rain. The best place to see the storm and be seen. |
| [[Carriage House\|Carriage House]] | Ground / Exterior | Exterior | A separate building for the old car, the tools, and the chauffeur's secrets. The headlights can be seen from the lodge. |
