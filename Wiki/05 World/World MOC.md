## The house in one paragraph

Blackwood Lodge is a four-level Gothic manor built over something older. The ground floor is public: foyer, ballroom, dining room, library, conservatory, kitchen, study, chapel. The first floor is private: archive, master suite, guest rooms, nursery, balcony. The attic is memory: trunks, servants, bell tower, observatory, linen. The cellar is the truth: wine, boiler, vault, crypt stair. The house changes one wing at midnight. The storm keeps everyone inside. The gate only opens for the Hollow Word.

## Design rules for every room

1. **Two routes.** Every room has at least two ways in or out, or a key that is not behind its own lock.
2. **One clue, one secret.** A room may carry more than one clue, but it must have one strong reason to enter.
3. **Readable in blackout.** Every critical prop has a lit fallback and a text card.
4. **No gore.** Blood is a stain, a ribbon, or a smear.
5. **The house is neutral.** It pressures everyone; it does not take sides.
6. **The killer can use it.** Every room has a killer advantage.
7. **The investigator can use it.** Every room has an investigator advantage.
8. **The room remembers.** Every room changes at midnight in a small, visible way.
