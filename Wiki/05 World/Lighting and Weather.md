# Lighting and Weather

> [!info] Design rule
> Light is information. Every light source tells the player where to go, what is safe, or what is about to happen. If a light is only decoration, it is not doing its job.

## The storm

The storm is the match's clock. It has four states:

| State | In-game time | Look | Sound | Gameplay |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Arrival** | 6:00–7:00 PM | Blue-grey dusk, rain on glass | Rain, distant thunder | Safe, exploratory |
| **Deep night** | 7:00 PM–12:00 AM | Black windows, warm practicals | Rain, house creaks | Investigation |
| **Midnight** | 12:00 AM | Lightning, lights out for 3 seconds | Thunder, bell | Shift, chapel/vault open |
| **Dawn** | 5:00–7:00 AM | Grey-blue, rain easing | Birds, distant train | Last Hunt, escape |

## Lightning

Lightning is a **free flash** every 45–90 seconds. It reveals:

- Silhouettes through windows.
- The layout of a dark room for one frame.
- A figure in the garden.
- The stained glass in the chapel.

Lightning is not a jump scare. It is a navigation tool with a startle. The flash has an accessibility toggle: reduced brightness, no strobe, longer fade.

## Blackout

The killer's **Flicker** sabotage kills the lights in one wing for 15 seconds. The blackout is not total:

- Emergency candles light the main route.
- The HUD shows a faint compass.
- Audio cues mark walls and doors.
- The UV torch still works.
- The player's own flashlight has a battery meter (proposed).

Blackout is a **readability test**. Every critical clue must have a lit fallback, a text card, or an audio cue. If a player cannot solve a task in blackout, the room fails.

## Weather as a mechanic

- **Rain** masks footsteps. The killer can move faster without being heard.
- **Thunder** covers door sounds. Players learn to move on the thunder.
- **Wind** slams doors and moves curtains. It creates false tells.
- **Cold** makes breath visible. The killer's cold spot sabotage uses this.
- **Mud** leaves footprints. The Conservatory and the woods path are tracking zones.
- **Flooding** blocks the lower driveway in the last 5 minutes, forcing the gate route.

## Accessibility

- **Photosensitivity:** lightning strobe off, flash fade 0.5s, no rapid flicker.
- **Low vision:** high-contrast mode, brighter practicals, outlined interactables.
- **Colorblind:** all light colors have a shape or icon cue; UV has a pattern, not just violet.
- **Motion:** camera shake off, lightning shake off.
- **Audio:** visual subtitles for thunder, bell, and whispers.
