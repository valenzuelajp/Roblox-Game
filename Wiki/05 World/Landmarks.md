# Landmarks

> [!spoiler] Design spoiler
> Some landmarks carry story secrets. This page is for designers and level builders.

## Why landmarks matter

A procedural map needs fixed points the player can navigate by. Landmarks are the **mental anchors** that make a changing house feel like a place, not a maze. They also carry the story without exposition.

## The landmarks

### The Front Gate

**Where:** the end of the driveway, south of the Foyer.
**What it is:** a wrought-iron gate with a brass letter lock, two stone pillars, and a road that washes out in the storm.
**Gameplay:** the exit. The Hollow Word opens it. A wrong word locks it for 30 seconds and rings the chapel bell once.
**Story:** the gate is the only way out. The cult knows it. The killer will camp it in the Last Hunt.
**Tell:** the gate has five brass letters. The lock is the only clean thing in the storm.

### The Driveway

**Where:** from the gate to the Foyer.
**What it is:** a gravel road lined with dead elms, a broken fountain, and a 1936 car buried in the mud.
**Gameplay:** the escape route. The Last Hunt often ends here.
**Story:** the nine guests arrived along this road. The car in the mud is theirs.
**Tell:** the gravel is too quiet; the rain makes it black.

### The Woods Path

**Where:** east of the Conservatory, into the Ashcombe woods.
**What it is:** a narrow path that appears only in the rain, marked by white stones and antler carvings.
**Gameplay:** a risky escape route. The path loops back to the gate if the player follows the white stones; it leads to the Hollow if they follow the antlers.
**Story:** the 1926 expedition entered here. The cult still uses it.
**Tell:** the white stones are warm. The antlers are cold.

### The Lake

**Where:** north of the lodge, below the terrace.
**What it is:** a black lake with a stone boathouse, a sunken rowboat, and a bell buoy that rings in the storm.
**Gameplay:** a possible post-launch escape route; at launch, it is a landmark and a sound cue.
**Story:** the lake is where the 1936 bodies were supposed to be found. They were not.
**Tell:** the water is too still. The bell buoy rings when there is no wind.

### The Chapel

**Where:** the east wing, ground floor.
**What it is:** a stone chapel with a stained-glass window of nine guests and one empty pane.
**Gameplay:** the ritual site. Sealed until midnight. Two entrances after midnight.
**Story:** the chapel was built over the Hollow. The ritual is the house's oldest room.
**Tell:** the chapel has no shadows. The bell is cracked.

### The Bell Tower

**Where:** the attic, above the chapel.
**What it is:** a stone tower with a cracked bronze bell and a new rope.
**Gameplay:** a vertical route, a signal, and a ritual anchor. The bell rings when the ritual begins and when the gate is wrong.
**Story:** the bell was removed in 1936, but it is still here.
**Tell:** the crack in the bell is shaped like a five-letter word.

### The Observatory

**Where:** the attic, above the archive.
**What it is:** a small dome with a brass telescope and a star chart that is actually the floor plan.
**Gameplay:** the zodiac puzzle and a map reveal.
**Story:** the cult tried to read the house as a constellation.
**Tell:** the telescope points at the chapel, not the sky.

### The Carriage House

**Where:** the west garden, a separate stone building.
**What it is:** two bays, a 1938 sedan, a workbench, and a chauffeur's log.
**Gameplay:** an isolated staging room, a tool cache, and a tunnel to the cellar.
**Story:** the guests arrived here. The car still runs.
**Tell:** the headlights turn on at midnight.

### The Hollow

**Where:** beneath the chapel, at the bottom of the crypt stair.
**What it is:** a place the game never fully shows: a cold room, a circle of stones, a sound like breathing, a word that is not spoken.
**Gameplay:** the ritual's source. The player never enters it in the launch version; it is the thing the ritual is trying to reach.
**Story:** the Hollow is real, a story, or a shared delusion. The game never confirms which.
**Tell:** the air is cold; the candles burn blue; the word is always on the tip of the tongue.

## Landmark placement rules

- The gate, driveway, and woods path are always present.
- The lake is present in 50% of seeds; it is a landmark, not a route.
- The chapel, bell tower, observatory, and carriage house are always present.
- The Hollow is never a playable room in the launch version.
- No landmark may be hidden behind a locked door without a second route.
