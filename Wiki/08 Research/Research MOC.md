# Research MOC

> [!info] What this folder is for
> Research is not canon. It is the evidence behind design choices. When a research note conflicts with [[Canonical Decisions]], the decision wins.

## Method

Each research page follows the same shape:

1. **Question** — what we needed to learn.
2. **Sources** — games, films, platform docs, playtests, or articles.
3. **Findings** — what the source actually shows.
4. **Design implications** — what we borrow, adapt, or reject.
5. **Open questions** — what we still do not know.

We do not cite a source we have not read. We do not treat a competitor's success as proof that a mechanic will work here. Playtests beat references.

## How to use research

- **For design:** use findings to justify a choice, then record the choice in [[Decision Log]].
- **For art:** use the reference tables in [[Noir and Horror References]] and [[Art Direction]].
- **For engineering:** use [[Roblox Platform Research]] and [[Procedural Generation Research]] before choosing a technical approach.
- **For publishing:** use [[Roblox Platform Research]] and [[Monetization and Progression Research]] before writing the store page.

## Source policy

- **Primary sources** are platform documentation, playtest notes, and our own telemetry.
- **Secondary sources** are articles, videos, and competitor analysis.
- **Inspiration** is not evidence. A film can inspire a mood; it cannot prove a mechanic.
- **Do not copy** copyrighted assets, names, logos, or distinctive characters. Borrow techniques, not identities.

## Open research questions

- **RQ-01:** Does the 30-minute continuous match hold attention without meeting phases?
- **RQ-02:** What is the minimum viable voice experience for a player without a microphone?
- **RQ-03:** How often should the map shift before players feel lost rather than surprised?
- **RQ-04:** Can a five-letter word be a satisfying endgame key without being guessable?
- **RQ-05:** What is the right punishment for a wrong accusation: social, economic, or both?
- **RQ-06:** How much lore can a 30-minute match carry before it slows the game?
- **RQ-07:** Which cosmetic categories sell without creating a silhouette advantage?
- **RQ-08:** What accessibility settings are used most, and which are ignored?
