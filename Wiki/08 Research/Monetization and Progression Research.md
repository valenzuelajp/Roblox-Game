# Monetization and Progression Research

## The question

What can we sell, and what must stay earned, to keep a social deduction game trustworthy?

## The principle

**Sell identity, not advantage.** A player can pay to look different. A player cannot pay to know more, survive longer, or win more.

This is locked in D-023: **no pay-to-win.**

## What Roblox allows

| Model | How it works | Fit for Blackwood Lodge |
|---|---|---|
| **Game passes** | Permanent access or perks | Cosmetics, private servers, casebook skins |
| **Developer products** | Consumable purchases | Cosmetic bundles, currency packs (earned currency only) |
| **Private servers** | Subscription | Strong fit for friend groups |
| **Premium payouts** | Engagement-based | Passive; no design impact |
| **Engagement-based payouts** | Qualified engagement | Passive; no design impact |
| **Ads** | In-experience ads | Reject; breaks immersion |
| **Loot boxes** | Randomized paid rewards | Reject; gambling risk |
| **Pay-to-win items** | Gameplay advantage | Reject; violates D-023 |

## What to sell

### Cosmetics

- Outfits (detective, noir, cult, staff, period).
- Hats and headwear.
- Glasses and masks (no gameplay effect).
- Gloves.
- Gun skins (no stat change).
- Lobby themes.
- Emotes.
- Casebook skins.
- Titles and nameplates.

### Private servers

- Friend groups can play without randoms.
- Host controls: voice on/off, difficulty, scenario pool.
- Subscription price set by Roblox; the host pays.

### Cosmetic bundles

- Seasonal bundles at a discount.
- Direct purchase, no randomness.
- Clear contents and prices.
- No limited-time FOMO pressure beyond the season.

## What not to sell

- Items, clues, bullets, health, speed, map reveals.
- Extra roles or killer slots.
- Answer hints.
- Skip-ritual tokens.
- Loot boxes or keys.
- Ads.
- Anything that changes the social read.

## Progression

### Match progression

- Cinders (earned only).
- Casebook entries.
- Badges.
- Detective Rank (proposed).

### Account progression

- Cosmetics.
- Titles.
- Lobby themes.
- Emotes.
- Casebook skins.
- Private server perks.

### The casebook

The casebook is the main collection:

- **Encountered:** the scenario appeared in a match.
- **Played:** the player was in the match.
- **Solved:** the team solved all four tasks.
- **Mastered:** the player solved a task without a wrong answer, or completed a challenge.

The casebook is a **story collection**, not a power track. It rewards replay without changing the odds.

## Pricing

| Tier | Robux | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Common** | 49–99 | Simple hats, glasses, gloves |
| **Rare** | 149–299 | Outfits, gun skins |
| **Epic** | 399–599 | Full outfits, lobby themes |
| **Legendary** | 799–999 | Seasonal bundles, casebook skins |

Prices are proposed. They must be tested against the audience and Roblox norms.

## Currency design

- **Shillings** are match-only. They reset every match.
- **Cinders** are earned-only. They persist.
- **Robux** is platform currency. It buys cosmetics, not Cinders.
- **No conversion** from Robux to Cinders. That would be pay-to-win.

## Fairness rules

- A paid cosmetic cannot change the silhouette in a way that hides the player.
- A paid cosmetic cannot change the color of a sigil, clue, or UI signal.
- A paid cosmetic cannot make a player harder to see in blackout.
- A paid cosmetic cannot play audio that masks footsteps or voice.
- A paid cosmetic cannot be a fake weapon or a fake role card.

## Trust signals

- Transparent drop rates (there are none; no loot boxes).
- Clear prices.
- No fake scarcity.
- No "buy now or lose it" pressure beyond a season.
- Refund policy as required by platform.
- No pay-to-win in the store description.
- A public "what we sell" page (the player wiki).

## Metrics

- Conversion rate.
- Average revenue per paying user.
- Cosmetic attach rate.
- Refund rate.
- Support tickets.
- Sentiment: "pay-to-win" mentions.
- Retention by spend tier (ensure no advantage).

## Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Cosmetics feel overpriced** | Test price tiers; offer bundles |
| **Season pass fatigue** | Direct purchase, no battle pass at launch |
| **Pay-to-win perception** | Publish the policy; no gameplay items |
| **Loot box regulation** | No loot boxes |
| **Private server abuse** | Host controls + report flow |
| **Currency confusion** | One earned currency (Cinders); Shillings are match-only |

## Open questions

- **RQ-21:** Does the audience prefer direct purchase or a season pass?
- **RQ-22:** What price point converts without feeling greedy?
- **RQ-23:** Should private servers be available at launch?
- **RQ-24:** How do we handle regional pricing and platform fees?
