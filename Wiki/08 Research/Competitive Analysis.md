# Competitive Analysis

## The question

What else is out there, and why would someone play Blackwood Lodge instead?

## The gap

Most social deduction games are either:

- **2D and meeting-based** (Among Us, Goose Goose Duck, Town of Salem) — fast, readable, but the meeting is the game.
- **3D and survival-based** (Project Winter, Dread Hunger, Deceit) — immersive, but long, complex, and voice-dependent.
- **Asymmetric horror** (Dead by Daylight, Friday the 13th) — chase, not deduction.

Blackwood Lodge sits in the gap:

- **3D, 30 minutes, no meetings.**
- **Investigation-first**, not task-first.
- **Voice recommended, text-first class.**
- **A changing map** and a **story-driven case**.
- **A ritual** that reveals the killer and turns the endgame into a chase.
- **Cosmetic-only, no pay-to-win.**

## Positioning statement

> **Blackwood Lodge is a 30-minute noir murder mystery for 6–10 players. No meetings. No vote. One of you staged the crime. Solve the case, survive the sigils, and escape before dawn.**

## Differentiators

1. **No meetings.** The clock never stops; the evidence board is the conversation.
2. **The house changes.** The map reconfigures every session and at midnight.
3. **Items change knowledge.** The shop is not cosmetic; it is how you turn a rumor into evidence.
4. **The ritual reveal.** The endgame exposes the killer and turns the table into a hunt.
5. **Noir, not gore.** The horror is in the staging, not the wound.
6. **Text-first playable.** Voice is a bonus, not a gate.

## Who it is for

- **Social deduction fans** who are tired of meeting spam.
- **Noir and mystery fans** who want a playable case.
- **Friend groups** who want a 30-minute story.
- **Streamers** who want a readable, reactive game.
- **Players without microphones** who are tired of being second-class.

## Who it is not for

- Players who want a fast twitch shooter.
- Players who want a 60-hour RPG.
- Players who want gore.
- Players who want pay-to-win progression.
- Players who refuse to read.

## Risks

| Risk | Why | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Among Us comparison** | "It's just 3D Among Us" | Emphasize investigation, no meetings, the house |
| **Too slow** | 30 minutes without meetings | Tension curve, sigils, midnight shift |
| **Too complex** | Four tasks + shop + puzzles | Onboarding, three options, free clues |
| **Voice dependency** | Social deduction is voice-native | Text fallback, pings, evidence board |
| **Content treadmill** | Ten scenarios may repeat | Procedural map + customization + seasonal cards |
| **Roblox discovery** | Crowded platform | Strong thumbnail, title, first 60 seconds |
