# Items and the Curio Shop

## The shop

- You enter a **private Curio Shop** before the match.
- You have **100 Shillings**.
- You have **four minutes**.
- You can buy **2–3 items**.
- The **team purchase board** shows how many of each item the team already has. It does not show names.
- Unspent Shillings convert to **Cinders** at the end of the match.

> [!tip] Buy for the gap
> If the team already has three cameras, buy something else. The board is there to prevent five people from buying the same tool.

## The ten investigator items

| Item | Cost | Charges | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | 25 | 3 | Photographs a scene. The flash reveals details that are invisible to the naked eye: plaques, hidden writing, and staging marks. |
| **Notebook** | 20 | ∞ | Records testimony and clues. Cross-references names, dates, and statements. |
| **Magnifying Glass** | 25 | ∞ | Examines wounds, fibers, and small traces. Reveals the detail that does not match the obvious story. |
| **Fingerprint Kit** | 30 | 2 | Lifts prints from props and weapons. Can match a print to a person. |
| **UV Torch** | 30 | 4 | Reveals blood trails, hidden sigils, and writing that is invisible in normal light. |
| **Compass** | 20 | ∞ | Points toward the nearest sigil or ritual anchor. Does not reveal the killer. |
| **Cipher Wheel** | 30 | ∞ | Decodes ciphers, zodiac messages, and blood writing. |
| **Recorder** | 35 | 3 | Records and replays testimony and ambient audio. Can catch a lie. |
| **Lockpick** | 25 | 2 | Opens locked doors and containers. Does not open the vault or the front gate. |
| **Map** | 25 | ∞ | Shows the current floor layout and the rooms you have explored. Does not show hidden clues. |

## Which items to buy

There is no single correct build. The team needs coverage:

- **Victim task:** Camera or Notebook.
- **Method task:** Magnifying Glass or Fingerprint Kit.
- **Scene task:** UV Torch or Compass.
- **Motive task:** Cipher Wheel or Recorder.
- **Utility:** Lockpick or Map.

A balanced team buys one item from each row. A greedy team buys five cameras and guesses.

## Item rules

- Items are **durable** for the match.
- Items **drop on death** and can be picked up.
- Items can be **traded** with other players (proposed feature; check the current patch notes).
- The killer can buy from a **black market** tab. Their items are not on the team board.
- Items never change your health, speed, or bullet damage. They change what you can **see and know**.

## The killer's shop

The killer sees the same shop, plus a hidden black-market tab. Black-market items are for sabotage, not direct damage. They may include:

- A forged document.
- Gloves that hide prints.
- Wire cutters.
- A cult sigil.
- A whisper mask.

The killer can buy at most **two** black-market items. They cannot buy guns or the answers.

## Money tips

- **Do not spend everything.** Unspent Shillings become Cinders.
- **Do not buy a duplicate** unless the item has limited charges and the team needs a backup.
- **Buy the item your team is missing**, not the item you personally like.
- **The free clue is free.** If you are broke, you can still solve the case — you will just have to guess between two answers.

## No pay-to-win

Everything in the shop is bought with **Shillings earned in the match**. Robux cannot buy Shillings, items, bullets, clues, or health. Cosmetics are the only Robux purchase.
