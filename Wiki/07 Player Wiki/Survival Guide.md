# Survival Guide

> [!tip] No spoilers
> This page gives tactics, not answers. Every match has a different crime.

## Before the match

- **Use a headset if you can.** Voice is recommended, not required. Text works.
- **Read the four task names.** Victim, Method, Scene, Motive. You will see them all night.
- **Decide what you are good at.** Searching, puzzles, talking, or fighting. Buy for that.
- **Do not spend all 100 Shillings on one item.** Two cheap tools usually beat one expensive one.

## The first five minutes

1. **Stay with the group until the first body is found.** The killer cannot mark in the first minute.
2. **Listen to the arrival scene.** The staff say one true thing and one lie. Write it down.
3. **Find the evidence board in the foyer.** It is the team's shared memory.
4. **Do not accuse anyone yet.** The first accusation is usually wrong.

## Investigator tips

### Search smart

- Search **containers**, not rooms. Drawers, books, coats, clocks, urns, floorboards.
- The free clue is usually in a **common** container. The item clue is usually **hidden**.
- If a room feels too clean, look behind the furniture.
- If a room feels too staged, look at what is missing.

### Buy for coverage

- The team needs at least one of each clue item: Camera/Notebook, Magnifying Glass, UV Torch, Cipher Wheel/Recorder, Ritual Anchor.
- Check the **team purchase board** before you buy. It shows counts, not names.
- A duplicate is not wasted: items drop on death and can be traded.

### Answer carefully

- A free clue eliminates **one** wrong option. You still have a 50/50.
- Do not submit until you have the item clue or a strong consensus.
- A wrong answer costs Cinders and may plant a false letter. It also tells the killer you are close.

### Survive the sigil

- When someone is marked, **everyone sees the sigil**. Call its location.
- Split: one solver, one lookout. The killer can defend the sigil.
- The puzzle is medium difficulty. If you are not good at puzzles, guard the solver.
- The victim should run toward the sigil, not away from it. Their life depends on the solver.

### Use the clock

- **6:00–14:00:** explore, find free clues, buy nothing else.
- **14:00:** the lodge shifts. Re-orient. The chapel and vault open.
- **14:00–24:00:** item clues, second pistol seals, ritual prep.
- **24:00–28:00:** ritual, reveal, Last Hunt. Do not start the ritual alone.
- **28:00–30:00:** escape. The gate needs the Hollow Word.

### Endgame

- The ritual reveals the killers. After that, the match is a chase, not a mystery.
- **Detain** is the 100% path. **Shoot** is faster but caps the reward at 50%.
- Do not shoot an innocent. You will get two sigils and drop the gun.
- If the killers are both detained, you win. Guard the cellar.

## Killer tips

> [!note] Spoiler-free
> These are general tactics. The specific scenario is different every match.

- **Be the most helpful person in the room.** The killer who solves clues is the last one suspected.
- **Stage the scene during the shop.** You have four minutes. Do not rush the customization.
- **Mark the solver, not the talker.** The person who finds clues is the real threat.
- **Place sigils far from the victim, not far from everyone.** A sigil nobody can reach is suspicious; a sigil everybody can reach is useless.
- **Use one sabotage at the right time.** A blackout during the ritual is worth more than five blackouts in the first minute.
- **Know when to stop hiding.** After the reveal, the Last Hunt favors you. Commit.

## Communication

- **Say what you found, not what you think.** "A torn ledger with a circled debt" is evidence. "It's definitely money" is a guess.
- **Repeat important clues in text** if voice is chaotic.
- **Use pings.** A ping is faster than a paragraph.
- **Do not stream-snipe.** If you are streaming, use a delay.
- **Do not ghost-leak.** Dead players cannot talk to the living. Respect it.

## Common mistakes

| Mistake | Why it hurts | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Submitting the first guess | Wrong answers cost Cinders and plant false letters | Wait for the item clue |
| Buying five cameras | No one can decode the motive | Check the team board |
| Ignoring the sigil | A teammate dies | Call it and split roles |
| Starting the ritual alone | The killer gets a free interrupt | Guard all three anchors |
| Shooting the first suspect | Innocent shot = two sigils + dropped gun | Detain or wait for the reveal |
| Staying in one wing | The midnight shift changes the map | Re-orient at 14:00 |
| Trusting the free clue | It is incomplete, not wrong | Use it to eliminate, not to convict |

## Accessibility

- You can play entirely with text. Voice is never required.
- Turn on **subtitles** and **visual cues** in Settings.
- Use **+50% puzzle time** and **+15s sigil time** if you need it.
- Remap controls and adjust motion/photosensitivity settings.
