## What this wiki is

- A guide to the rules, controls, and systems.
- A place to learn without ruining the mystery.
- A reference for players who use text instead of voice.
- A living document updated with each patch.

## What this wiki is not

- A list of scenario answers.
- A map of every clue.
- A spoiler for the story.
- A place to harass other players.

## The spoiler promise

If a page would tell you **who the killer is**, **what the scenario is**, or **what the Hollow Word is**, it does not belong here. Those answers are different every match. The fun is in the investigation.
