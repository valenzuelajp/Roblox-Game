# About the Game

> [!quote] One-line pitch
> A 30-minute noir social-deduction game where ten guests, one staged crime, and a house that changes its mind.

## What it is

**Blackwood Lodge** is a social-deduction horror game for **6–10 players** on Roblox. One or two players are cultists hiding among a group of investigators. The investigators solve a four-part crime, perform a ritual, and try to escape the lodge before dawn. The killers hunt them, sabotage the house, and try to keep everyone inside until 7:00 AM.

The game is designed for **one continuous 30-minute match** with **no meeting phases**. Communication is open voice and text. Every player has something to do: search, decode, negotiate, rescue, fight, or lie.

## Who it is for

- Fans of social deduction who want more **investigation** and less **meeting room**.
- Fans of noir and gothic horror who want atmosphere without gore.
- Players who prefer **voice-optional** games with strong text support.
- Streamers who want a match that produces a story every time.

## What makes it different

| Most social-deduction games | Blackwood Lodge |
|---|---|
| Meeting phases | No meetings; the clock never stops |
| Random accusations | Physical evidence and a shared Case File |
| Fixed maps | A procedurally generated lodge that shifts at midnight |
| One killer ability | Marks, sigils, sabotage, partners, and a second pistol |
| Death is the end | Ghosts can watch, learn, and coach in dead chat |
| Cosmetics are the only progression | A casebook that records every crime you solve |

## Platform and price

- **Platform:** Roblox (PC, Mac, mobile, console where supported).
- **Price:** Free to play.
- **Monetization:** Cosmetic-only. No pay-to-win, no loot boxes, no ads.
- **Private servers:** Available for groups of 6–10.
- **Voice chat:** Recommended, never required.

## Content rating

The game contains:

- Stylized blood and implied violence.
- Dark rooms, sudden sounds, and psychological horror.
- No gore, sexual content, or real-world hate symbols.

A content warning is shown before the first match. Startle effects and flashing can be reduced in Settings.

## The team

Blackwood Lodge is an independent project built in Roblox Studio with Luau. The design, story, and systems are documented in a private design vault. The player wiki is a separate, spoiler-free export.

**Credits:**

- Design and writing: the Blackwood Lodge team.
- Art direction: noir/gothic reference board.
- Audio direction: storm, house, and ritual soundscape.
- Community: playtesters and moderators.

## Contact
