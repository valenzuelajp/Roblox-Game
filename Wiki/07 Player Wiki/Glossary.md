# Glossary

> [!info] No spoilers
> These definitions describe mechanics, not the story's secrets.

## A–F

**Anchor (map):** A room that appears in every match. The eight anchors are Foyer, Ballroom, Kitchen, Study, Chapel, Archive, Vault, and Attic.

**Anchor (ritual):** One of the three objects that must be held during the ritual: the altar word, the floor sigil, and the bell rope.

**Case File:** The shared notebook of four tasks: Victim, Method, Scene, Motive.

**Channeler:** The player who performs the 30-second ritual. Anyone can volunteer.

**Cinders:** The earned currency. Used for cosmetics and casebook unlocks. Never sold for Robux.

**Clue (free):** A low-quality clue that is always available. It eliminates one wrong answer.

**Clue (item):** A definitive clue that requires a specific investigator item.

**Curio Shop:** The private pre-match shop where players spend 100 Shillings.

## G–L

**Ghost:** A dead player. Can fly, watch, and use dead chat. Cannot affect the living.

**Ghost Blueprint:** A reward from the Motive task. Shows the original 1926 layout and hidden passages.

**Hollow Word:** The five-letter word that opens the front gate. Four letters from tasks, one from the ritual.

**Investigator:** A non-killer player. 1 HP. Solves the case, stops the killers, escapes.

**Item clue:** See "Clue (item)."

**Killer:** The hidden cultist. 1–2 per match. 2 HP. Marks investigators, sabotages the lodge, prevents escape.

**Last Hunt:** The 60-second endgame after the ritual. Killers get no mark cooldown, a speed boost, and a short wallhack.

## M–R

**Mark:** The killer's targeting ability. Creates a visible sigil and a countdown.

**Midnight shift:** The map change at 14 minutes. One wing reconfigures; chapel and vault open.

**Modular room:** A room drawn from a pool and placed by the seed. Changes every match.

**Pistol (first):** Awarded when the first task is solved. Four bullets.

**Pistol (second):** Behind three seals. Claimed from the vault cabinet. Four bullets.

**Puzzle family:** One of five puzzle types: zodiac, binary, blood cipher, rune alignment, knot/pattern.

**Ritual:** The 30-second channel that reveals the killers, grants the fifth letter, and unlocks the exit.

## S–Z

**Seal:** One of three puzzles that open the vault cabinet.

**Shillings:** The pre-match currency. 100 per player. Unspent Shillings become Cinders.

**Sigil:** The physical mark left by the killer. Anyone can break it by solving a puzzle.

**Task:** One of the four Case File questions.

**Team purchase board:** A shop display that shows how many of each item the team has bought, without names.

**Vault:** The sealed room with the second pistol. Opens after midnight.

**Witness:** A staff NPC who gives testimony. Each witness has one true thing and one lie.

**Zodiac puzzle:** A puzzle family using constellations, star signs, and dates.
