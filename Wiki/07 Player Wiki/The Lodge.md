# The Lodge

> [!warning] The house is not stable
> The layout changes every match. The rooms below are the **anchors** — the places you can count on. The rooms between them move.

## The estate

Blackwood Lodge sits at the end of a washed-out road in the Ashcombe woods. The storm cuts the road behind you. The front gate is the only way out, and it is locked with a five-letter brass lock.

The lodge has **four levels**:

| Level | What is here | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Cellar** | Wine cellar, boiler room, vault, crypt stair | The vault holds the second pistol. The crypt stair leads to the chapel. |
| **Ground** | Foyer, ballroom, kitchen, study, dining room, library, conservatory, chapel | The evidence board is in the foyer. The chapel is the ritual site. |
| **First** | Archive, master suite, guest rooms, balcony, nursery | The archive holds the case history. The guest rooms hold personal clues. |
| **Attic** | Attic, servant quarters, bell tower, observatory | The bell tower controls the ritual bell. The observatory sees the gate. |

## The anchors

These rooms are in every match. Their **connections** change.

### Foyer

The first room. A grand staircase, a chandelier, a portrait with a cut-out face, and the **evidence board**. The front door is behind you. It does not open until the Hollow Word is spoken.

**What to do here:** read the board, check the guest book, split into search teams.

### Ballroom

Dust sheets, a grand piano, a floor that reflects too much. The piano is in tune. No one has tuned it in ten years.

**What to do here:** check the piano, the stage, the coat check, the balcony door.

### Kitchen

A cold stove, ten place settings, one chair pushed in. The cellar door is here. The cook is here, and the cook knows more than she says.

**What to do here:** search the pantry, the icebox, the dumbwaiter, the cellar door.

### Study

A desk, a typewriter, a wall of books, a safe. The victim's papers are here. The room is too clean in one corner.

**What to do here:** check the desk, the safe, the fireplace, the rug.

### Chapel

The ritual site. Nine stained-glass figures and one empty pane. Ten pews, nine hymnals. The altar has five candles; the fifth is black. The bell rope is here.

**What to do here:** perform the ritual after midnight, guard the three anchors, ring the bell.

### Archive

The case history. Filing cabinets, a cyanotype map, a shelf of ledgers. The 1926 map is filed under 1936.

**What to do here:** find the Ghost Blueprint, check the ledgers, compare the maps.

### Vault

Behind three seals. The second pistol is inside. The vault is sealed until midnight. The first player to interact with the cabinet claims the gun.

**What to do here:** solve the three seals, claim the pistol, guard the cabinet.

### Attic

The house's memory. A child's toy, a trunk, a chimney, a bell rope. The attic is the highest point and the quietest.

**What to do here:** check the trunk, the chimney, the bell rope, the roof access.

## Modular rooms

Between the anchors are **12–16 modular rooms**. They are drawn from a pool and placed by the seed. You may see:

- Guest rooms, a nursery, a linen room, a balcony.
- A conservatory, a library, a dining room, a carriage house.
- A wine cellar, a boiler room, a crypt stair, a servant quarters.
- A bell tower, an observatory, a hidden passage.

The modular rooms are where most clues hide. They are also where the killer has the advantage.

## The midnight shift

At **midnight in-game (about 14 minutes real time)**, the lodge changes:

- One wing reconfigures. A corridor is longer. A door is where a wall was.
- The lights go out for three seconds.
- The chapel and the vault unseal.
- The map updates on your compass and map item.
- The Ghost Blueprint does **not** update. It shows the 1926 house, not the current one.
- Clues, the body, and the staging room do **not** move.

The shift is not random. The house is correcting the story. If a room feels wrong after midnight, trust your memory, not the wallpaper.

## The grounds

- **The front gate:** the only exit. The brass lock takes the Hollow Word.
- **The driveway:** the storm washes it out. The car does not start.
- **The woods path:** appears in the rain. It leads to the lake, not to town.
- **The lake:** black water, a boathouse, a rowboat with one oar.
- **The carriage house:** a car, a workshop, a smell of gasoline.
- **The chapel graveyard:** nine headstones, one empty plot.

## The house's rules

1. **The house is not on anyone's side.** It does not help the killer or the investigators.
2. **The house remembers.** Props move, portraits change, and rooms shift at midnight.
3. **The house wants the ritual completed.** It will make the ritual easier to find and harder to finish.
4. **The house does not let you leave without the word.** The gate is the final puzzle.
5. **The house is quiet when it is done.** If the rain stops, something has ended.
