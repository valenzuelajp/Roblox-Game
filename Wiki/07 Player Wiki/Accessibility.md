# Accessibility

> [!important] Voice is never required
> Every social action, clue, puzzle, and vote has a text equivalent. You can play the entire game without a microphone.

## Vision

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Subtitles** | Shows all voice lines, NPC dialogue, and important sounds as text. |
| **Speaker labels** | Tells you who is speaking, including "nearby" and "unknown." |
| **Visual sound cues** | Shows a direction indicator for footsteps, gunshots, bells, and whispers. |
| **Colorblind palettes** | Deuteranopia, protanopia, tritanopia, and high-contrast. |
| **High-contrast UI** | Stronger borders, larger text, less transparency. |
| **UI scale** | 80%–150%. |
| **Text size** | Small, medium, large, extra large. |
| **Screen reader** | Reads menus, the Case File, the evidence board, and chat. Puzzle text is read aloud. |
| **Reduce flashing** | Removes lightning strobes and rapid light changes. |
| **Reduce motion** | Removes camera shake, head bob, and UI animation. |

Color is never the only signal. Every color-coded clue also has a shape, a label, or a pattern.

## Hearing

- **Subtitles** cover voice chat, NPCs, and ambient cues.
- **Visual sound cues** show a direction and a symbol for key sounds.
- **Mono audio** mixes left and right into one channel.
- **Voice chat is optional.** Use text chat, pings, and the evidence board.
- **Important audio is repeated visually.** The ritual bell, the sigil whisper, and the gate lock all have screen indicators.

## Motor

- **Full remapping** for keyboard, mouse, and gamepad.
- **Toggle vs hold** for aim, sprint, and interact.
- **Hold duration** can be reduced or increased.
- **One-button mode** for interactions that normally require a hold.
- **Auto-search** option: search takes 2 seconds without holding.
- **Mobile:** large touch targets, adjustable joystick, tap-to-interact, and a simplified HUD.
- **Gamepad:** full support with button prompts.

## Cognitive

- **Objective tracker** always shows the next step.
- **Case File summaries** repeat the four tasks in plain language.
- **Hint system** gives a nudge before a solution.
- **Puzzle time** can be increased by 50%.
- **Sigil timer** can be increased by 15 seconds.
- **Tutorial** can be replayed from the menu.
- **Glossary** explains every term in the game.

## Speech and communication

- **Text chat** works everywhere.
- **Quick chat** has premade lines: "I found a clue," "Sigil here," "Need help," "Follow me," "I suspect..."
- **Ping wheel** marks locations, items, suspects, and danger.
- **Emotes** cover yes, no, wait, help, thanks, and danger.
- **Voice-to-text** is supported on platforms where Roblox provides it.
- **No mechanic requires a microphone.**

## Difficulty

Blackwood Lodge is designed to be **medium difficulty**. If you want it easier:

- Turn on **+50% puzzle time**.
- Turn on **+15s sigil time**.
- Turn on **hint markers** for the next objective.
- Play the **guided first match**.
- Use the **practice range** for puzzles and shooting.

If you want it harder:

- Turn off hints.
- Reduce puzzle time.
- Play in a private server with friends.

## Photosensitivity and motion

- **Reduce flashing** removes lightning strobes.
- **Reduce motion** removes camera shake and head bob.
- **Static rain** removes the animated rain overlay.
- **No jump scares** are required to progress. Startle effects can be reduced or disabled.

## Platform notes

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| **PC** | Full keyboard/mouse, gamepad, voice, text. |
| **Mac** | Roblox client; same settings. |
| **Mobile** | Touch controls, simplified HUD, text chat, optional voice where supported. |
| **Console** | Gamepad, text chat with on-screen keyboard, voice where supported. |
| **VR** | Not supported at launch. |

## Reporting an accessibility barrier

If a setting does not work, or a puzzle is impossible with your setup, report it:

1. Open **Settings → Help → Report a Problem**.
2. Choose **Accessibility**.
3. Describe the barrier and the room or puzzle.
4. Include your platform and input device.

Accessibility reports are treated as bugs, not suggestions.
