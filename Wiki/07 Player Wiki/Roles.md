# Roles

## Investigator

**You are trying to solve a crime and survive the night.**

- **Goal:** solve the four Case File tasks, complete the ritual, learn the Hollow Word, stop the killers if you can, and escape before dawn.
- **Health:** 1 HP. One bullet kills you.
- **Tools:** 100 Shillings in the Curio Shop, plus anything you find or trade.
- **Voice:** recommended, never required.
- **Win:** escape. Stopping the killers earns the full reward.

**Tips:**
- Search in pairs. A sigil is easier to break when someone is nearby.
- Share free clues early. They are safe and always eliminate one option.
- Do not hoard items. A camera in your pocket does not help the team.
- Watch who knows too much, who avoids the chapel, and who pushes a wrong answer.

## Killer

**You are a cultist planted among the investigators.**

- **Goal:** protect the staged crime, thin the house, and prevent escape until dawn.
- **Health:** 2 HP. You survive one bullet.
- **Power:** mark investigators, sabotage the lodge, and use the black market.
- **Partners:** at 8–10 players there are two killers. They know each other and share a mark cooldown.
- **Win:** reach parity, disarm the investigators, or keep everyone inside until 7:00 AM.

**Tips:**
- Do not overact. The best killers are the most helpful person in the room.
- Your false clue is a scalpel, not a hammer. Plant it where it will be found.
- Mark the player who is holding the team together, not the loudest one.
- The ritual reveals you. Save something for the Last Hunt.

> [!note] No spoilers
> The killer's scenario, staging options, and true answers are different every match. This wiki will not tell you who the killer is — that is the game.

## Channeler

**The channeler is the investigator who performs the ritual.**

- The role is **voluntary**, not assigned at the start.
- The channeler must hold the ritual for **30 seconds**.
- The killer can interrupt by marking the channeler or breaking an anchor.
- The channeler is vulnerable. Protect them.
- If the channeler is interrupted, the ritual can be retried after a short cooldown.

**Tips:**
- Do not channel alone. Bring at least one guard.
- The chapel has two entrances. Watch both.
- If you are marked while channeling, break the sigil first. The ritual can wait.

## Ghost

**Dead players become ghosts.**

- Ghosts can fly around the lodge and watch the living.
- Ghosts **cannot** speak to the living, interact with props, vote, or solve puzzles.
- Ghosts have a **dead-only chat**.
- Ghosts can watch the killer only after the ritual reveals them.
- Ghosts earn partial Cinders for the match.

**Tips:**
- Do not leak information to living players in voice or text. It is cheating.
- Use dead chat to help other ghosts understand the match.
- Spectate the ritual. It is the best show in the house.

## Role assignment

| Players | Killers |
|---|---|
| 6–7 | 1 killer |
| 8–10 | 2 killers |

Roles are assigned randomly and secretly at the start of the match. The first pistol is never given to a killer.

## What not to do

- **Do not metagame.** Do not join a call with players in another match, stream snipe, or share your screen to reveal your role.
- **Do not ghost-leak.** Dead players cannot tell living players where the killer is.
- **Do not harass.** Accusing someone in-game is part of the game. Harassment is not.
- **Do not spoil.** Do not post scenario answers or the Hollow Word in public chat.
