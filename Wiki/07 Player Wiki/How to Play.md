# How to Play

## The short version

You are one of **6–10 guests** at Blackwood Lodge. **One or two of you are cultists.** The rest are investigators.

- **Investigators** solve four Case File tasks, perform a ritual, learn the Hollow Word, and escape before 7:00 AM.
- **Killers** staged the crime before the match. They mark investigators, sabotage the search, and try to keep everyone inside until dawn.

There are **no meetings**. The match is one continuous 30-minute night.

## The clock

| In-game time | What happens |
|---|---|
| **6:00 PM** | The Curio Shop. Everyone buys items in a private instance. The killer stages the crime. |
| **7:00 PM** | Arrival. The storm cuts the road. The host is missing. The first body is found. |
| **8:00–11:00 PM** | Investigation I. Search, interview, and solve tasks. |
| **Midnight** | The lodge shifts. One wing changes. The chapel and vault open. |
| **12:00–5:00 AM** | Investigation II. The second pistol race. The killer gets bolder. |
| **5:00–6:30 AM** | The ritual. The killers are revealed. The Last Hunt begins. |
| **6:30–7:00 AM** | Escape. Reach the gate with the Hollow Word. |
| **7:00 AM** | Dawn. If you have not escaped, the cult wins. |

## The Case File

The Case File has **four tasks**:

1. **Victim** — Who was killed?
2. **Method** — How were they killed?
3. **Scene** — Where and how was the scene staged?
4. **Motive** — Why did the cult want them dead?

Each task has **three possible answers**. You have two ways to find the truth:

- **Free clue:** always available, low quality. It eliminates **one wrong answer**.
- **Item clue:** requires the right investigator item. It confirms the **correct answer**.

Any living player can submit an answer. The first submission locks that task.

- **Correct:** you earn a letter of the Hollow Word, Cinders, and case progress.
- **Wrong:** the task locks for 60 seconds, you lose Cinders, and a false letter may be planted.

The four task letters plus the ritual letter spell the **Hollow Word**. The word opens the front gate.

Read [[The Case File]] for the full guide.

## The sigil

The killer does not stab you in the dark. They **mark** you.

1. The killer marks you.
2. A **visible sigil** appears somewhere in the lodge.
3. A countdown begins.
4. **Anyone** can break the sigil by solving a puzzle.
5. If the sigil is not broken in time, you die.

The sigil is a race, not a cutscene. Call it out. Split up. Solve it.

Read [[Survival Guide]] for the full sigil loop.

## The pistols

There are two pistols in the match.

- **First pistol:** awarded when the first Case File task is solved. Four bullets.
- **Second pistol:** hidden behind three seals on three floors. Solve all three, open the cabinet, and claim it. Four bullets.

Investigators have **1 HP**. Killers have **2 HP**. One bullet deals one damage.

- Shooting the **right** killer stops them.
- Shooting an **innocent** kills them. You receive **two sigils** and drop the gun.

Read [[Survival Guide]] for pistol rules, detain, and friendly fire.

## The ritual

After all four tasks are solved, the chapel opens at midnight. One player volunteers as the **channeler**. The channeler holds the ritual for **30 seconds**.

The killer can interrupt by marking the channeler or breaking an anchor. The ritual is retryable.

When the ritual completes:

- Every killer is revealed.
- You receive the fifth letter.
- The exit unlocks.
- The **Last Hunt** begins: the killers get a short window of speed and aggression.

## How to win

**Investigators win** by escaping before 7:00 AM.

- **100% reward:** all four tasks solved, ritual completed, killers stopped.
- **50% reward:** killers stopped, but the case is incomplete.
- **25% reward:** escape without stopping the killers.

**Killers win** by reaching parity, disarming the investigators, or keeping everyone inside until dawn.

## First-match tips

- **Buy for the team, not yourself.** The team purchase board shows what everyone has.
- **Read the free clue first.** It always eliminates one wrong answer.
- **Call out sigils immediately.** A sigil is not a private problem.
- **Do not shoot without evidence.** An innocent shot can lose the match.
- **Use text if you do not use voice.** Ping, chat, and the evidence board are enough.
- **Watch the clock.** The lodge is big; do not leave the ritual to the last minute.
