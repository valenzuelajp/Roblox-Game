# FAQ

> [!tip] Still stuck?
> Ask in the community Discord or use the in-game Help menu. Do not ask for scenario answers; that is a spoiler.

## The basics

**What is Blackwood Lodge?**
A social-deduction horror game for 6–10 players. Investigators solve a staged crime while one or two killers hunt them. One 30-minute match, no meetings, open voice and text.

**How long is a match?**
30 minutes. The in-game clock runs from 6:00 PM to 7:00 AM.

**How many players?**
6–10. At 6–7 there is one killer. At 8–10 there are two partnered killers.

**Is it free?**
Yes. The game is free to play. Cosmetics are optional and never affect gameplay.

**Does it require voice chat?**
No. Voice is recommended because it is a social game, but every action works in text.

**Is it scary?**
It is atmospheric horror, not gore. Stylized blood, dark rooms, sudden sounds. You can reduce flashing and startle effects.

**Can I play solo?**
No. You need at least 6 players. Private servers need 6–10.

**Can I play with friends?**
Yes. Party up, then queue together. Private servers are available.

## Roles

**How is the killer chosen?**
Secretly, by the server, at the start of the match. The killer is never announced until the ritual.

**Can I choose to be the killer?**
No. Role assignment is random, with a preference system planned for private servers.

**What happens when I die?**
You become a ghost. You can fly around, watch, and talk in dead chat. You cannot talk to the living, vote, or interact.

**Can I be revived?**
No. Death is permanent for the match. The ritual does not revive players.

**What is a channeler?**
A volunteer who performs the 30-second ritual. The killer can interrupt by marking the channeler or breaking an anchor. Anyone can be the channeler.

## The Case File

**What are the four tasks?**
Victim, Method, Scene, Motive. Each has three answer options.

**What is the difference between a free clue and an item clue?**
The free clue is always available and eliminates one wrong option. The item clue requires the matching investigator item and confirms the truth.

**Can I solve a task without the item?**
Yes, but you are guessing between two options. A wrong answer costs Cinders and may plant a false letter.

**Who can submit an answer?**
Any living player, including the killer. The first submission locks the task.

**What happens if we get it wrong?**
The task locks for 60 seconds, the team loses Cinders, and there is a chance of a false Hollow Word letter. The killer is alerted.

**What is the Hollow Word?**
A five-letter word. Four letters come from the four tasks; the fifth comes from the ritual. It opens the front gate.

**Can we guess the word?**
You can try. A wrong word locks the gate for 30 seconds and rings a bell the killer can hear.

## The ritual

**When can we do the ritual?**
After all four tasks are solved and the chapel is open (after midnight).

**How long does it take?**
30 seconds of channeling. The killer can interrupt by marking the channeler or breaking one of the three anchors.

**What happens if it is interrupted?**
The ritual stops. You can retry after 60 seconds. Three failures seal the chapel for 90 seconds, but the fifth letter can still be recovered under the altar with a UV torch.

**What does the ritual do?**
It reveals the killers to everyone, grants the fifth letter, unlocks the exit, and starts the Last Hunt.

## Items and the shop

**How much money do I start with?**
100 Shillings.

**How many items can I buy?**
Usually 2–3. Items cost 20–35 Shillings.

**Can I see what my team bought?**
The team purchase board shows counts by item type, not names.

**What happens to unspent Shillings?**
They convert to Cinders at the end of the match, 1:1 (proposed).

**Do items carry over between matches?**
No. Items are per match. Cosmetics and casebook progress carry over.

**Can the killer buy items?**
Yes. The killer has the same shop plus a small black market. Black-market items do not appear on the team board.

**Can I trade items?**
Trading is planned. It is not guaranteed at launch.

## Combat

**How much health do I have?**
Investigators have 1 HP. Killers have 2 HP. A bullet deals 1 damage.

**How many bullets does a pistol have?**
Four. There is no reload.

**How do I get the first pistol?**
The first task solved triggers it. The holder is random or voted (the final method is still being tested).

**How do I get the second pistol?**
Solve three seals, one per floor, after midnight. The first player to interact with the vault cabinet claims it.

**What happens if I shoot an innocent?**
The innocent dies, you get two sigils, and you drop the gun. You also lose the 100% reward path.

**Can I detain instead of shooting?**
Yes. Detain is the non-lethal path. It is harder, but it preserves the 100% reward.

## The map

**Why does the map change?**
Every match uses a new seed. Room placement, connections, secrets, and locks change.

**What is the midnight shift?**
At midnight, one wing reconfigures, the chapel and vault open, and the map updates. Clues and the body do not move.

**What is the Ghost Blueprint?**
A reward from the Motive task. It shows the original 1926 layout and the hidden ritual entrance. It does not show the current clue locations.

**Can I get lost?**
Yes. Use the compass, the map item, the signs, and voice/text callouts. The house is designed to be disorienting.

## Progression and monetization

**What are Cinders?**
The earned currency. They come from solving tasks, breaking sigils, surviving, stopping killers, and completing the ritual. They are capped per match.

**What can I buy with Cinders?**
Cosmetics, casebook skins, titles, and lobby themes. Never gameplay advantages.

**Can I buy power with Robux?**
No. Monetization is cosmetic-only. No items, bullets, health, clues, or map advantages are for sale.

**Are there loot boxes?**
No. Cosmetics are direct purchase.

**Is there a battle pass?**
Not at launch. Seasonal cosmetic collections may be added later.

## Technical

**What platforms are supported?**
PC, Mac, mobile, and console (where Roblox supports them). VR is not supported at launch.

**Can I reconnect?**
Yes, if the match is still running and your slot is open. You return with your items and health, but you may have missed clues.

**Does the game support cross-play?**
Yes, through Roblox.

**How do I report a bug?**
Settings → Help → Report a Problem. Include the room, the time, and what happened.

## Community

**Can I stream?**
Yes. Use a delay to avoid stream sniping. Do not spoil the scenario for your viewers.

**Can I make content about the game?**
Yes, with credit. Do not publish scenario answers or the Hollow Word list.

**Where do I report a player?**
Use the in-game player list → Report. See [[Reporting and Safety]].
