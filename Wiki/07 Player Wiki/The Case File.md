# The Case File

The Case File is the heart of the match. It has **four tasks** and one **ritual**.

## The four tasks

| # | Task | The question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Victim** | Who was killed? |
| 2 | **Method** | How were they killed? |
| 3 | **Scene** | Where and how was the scene staged? |
| 4 | **Motive** | Why did the cult want them dead? |

Each task has **three possible answers**. Only one is true.

## Two kinds of clues

### Free clue

- **Cost:** none.
- **Quality:** low. Incomplete, ambiguous, or contaminated.
- **Effect:** eliminates **one wrong answer**. Two options remain.
- **Where:** common containers, documents, and environmental details.

### Item clue

- **Cost:** the right investigator item.
- **Quality:** definitive. It reveals a physical detail only that item can see.
- **Effect:** confirms the **correct answer**.
- **Where:** hidden containers, locked rooms, and item-specific surfaces.

> [!important] The 50/50
> If you have the free clue but not the item, you are guessing between two answers. That is intentional. The item turns a guess into a conviction.

## Submitting an answer

- Any **living player** can submit — investigator or killer.
- The **first submission locks** that task.
- You can discuss before submitting, but the clock does not stop.
- The killer can submit a wrong answer to burn the task. Watch who is eager to answer.

### Correct answer

- +1 case progress.
- One letter of the **Hollow Word**.
- Cinders.
- A new entry on the evidence board.

### Wrong answer

- The task locks for **60 seconds**.
- You lose Cinders.
- A **false letter** may be planted (35% chance).
- The killer is alerted that the task was answered wrong.

## The letters

Each correct task gives you **one letter**. The ritual gives you the **fifth**.

The five letters spell the **Hollow Word**. The word opens the front gate.

- Letters are shown in the Case File.
- A false letter is indistinguishable from a true one until the gate rejects it.
- The evidence board can help you spot a contradiction.

## The ritual

After all four tasks are solved, the chapel opens at midnight.

1. Choose a **channeler**.
2. The channeler holds the altar for **30 seconds**.
3. The killer can interrupt by marking the channeler or breaking an anchor.
4. If the ritual completes, every killer is revealed, you get the fifth letter, and the exit unlocks.

The ritual is retryable. If it is interrupted, wait for the cooldown and try again.

## The evidence board

The foyer has a physical corkboard. Use it to:

- Pin clues and photographs.
- Connect related evidence with string.
- Compare testimonies.
- Track the four tasks and the letters.

The board is shared by the whole team. The killer can tamper with it once per match.

## Tips

- **Start with the free clue.** It is always available and always eliminates one option.
- **Do not submit early.** A wrong answer locks the task for 60 seconds.
- **Share the item clue.** The person with the camera does not have to be the person who submits.
- **Check the board.** Two clues that contradict each other are a lead.
- **Watch the killer's answers.** A killer who pushes a wrong answer is telling you something.

## What this page will not tell you

- Which answer is correct in your match.
- Where the clues are.
- What the Hollow Word is.
- Who the killer is.

Those are different every session. That is the game.
