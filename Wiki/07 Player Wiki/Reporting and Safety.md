# Reporting and Safety

> [!important] The short version
> Be decent. Play the game. Do not spoil the mystery. If someone breaks the rules, report them and move on.

## Community rules

1. **No harassment.** No slurs, threats, stalking, or targeted abuse.
2. **No sexual content.** The game is rated for a general audience.
3. **No hate symbols or extremist imagery.** The cult in the game is fictional.
4. **No real-world violence or doxxing.**
5. **No cheating.** No exploits, aimbots, wallhacks, speed hacks, or script injection.
6. **No ghost leaks.** Dead players may not tell living players what they see.
7. **No stream sniping.** Do not use a stream to find players.
8. **No spoilers.** Do not post scenario answers, Hollow Words, or ending details in public chat.
9. **No griefing.** Do not team with the killer as an investigator, block doorways on purpose, or sabotage your own team.
10. **No voice abuse.** No screaming, music, or soundboards over voice chat.

## What to do if someone breaks the rules

### In the moment

- **Mute** the player. The mute button is in the player list.
- **Block** the player if you do not want to match with them again.
- **Leave** the match if you feel unsafe. You will not be penalized.
- **Do not argue.** Arguing gives the harasser attention and ruins the match for everyone else.

### After the match

1. Open the **player list**.
2. Select the player.
3. Choose **Report**.
4. Pick a category: harassment, cheating, voice abuse, spoilers, or other.
5. Add a short description. Include the room, the time, and what happened.
6. Submit.

Reports go to the game's moderation team and, where required, to Roblox moderation. You can also report directly through Roblox.

## What happens after a report

| Severity | Example | First response | Repeat |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low** | Spoiler in chat, mild rudeness | Warning | Mute or temporary chat restriction |
| **Medium** | Harassment, griefing, voice spam | Temporary ban | Longer ban |
| **High** | Threats, hate speech, sexual content, doxxing | Permanent ban | Report to Roblox |
| **Critical** | Real-world danger, exploitation, illegal content | Immediate ban + platform report | Law enforcement where required |

We do not publish individual moderation actions. You will receive a confirmation that the report was reviewed, not the details.

## Voice chat safety

- Voice chat is **opt-in** and governed by Roblox's voice safety systems.
- You can mute individual players at any time.
- You can play entirely in text.
- Do not share personal information over voice.
- Do not record other players without consent.
- If someone is underage or behaving inappropriately, report them.

## Cheating and exploits

Cheating includes:

- Speed, fly, teleport, or noclip exploits.
- Aimbots or automatic puzzle solvers.
- Wallhacks or ESP.
- Script injection or client modification.
- Boosting or win-trading.
- Sharing scenario answers with the killer.

If you see cheating, report it with the player's name and a description. Do not try to counter-cheat or harass the player.

## Spoilers

The mystery is the product. Do not post:

- Scenario answers.
- Hollow Words.
- Killer identities from a specific match.
- Ending details.
- The nine guests' identities or fates.

Spoilers in public chat are a **low-severity** offense: warning first, then a chat restriction. Spoilers in the player wiki are a documentation bug; report them through the wiki's issue link.

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation action was a mistake:

1. Use the **Appeal** link in the moderation notice.
2. Include your username, the match ID, and what you think happened.
3. Wait for a response. Do not open multiple appeals.

Appeals are reviewed by a human. Threats or abuse in an appeal are grounds for a longer ban.

## Support

- **Game bugs:** Settings → Help → Report a Problem.
- **Accessibility barriers:** Settings → Help → Report a Problem → Accessibility.
- **Account issues:** Roblox Support.
- **Wiki corrections:** use the wiki's issue link on [[Player Wiki MOC]].
