# Welcome to Blackwood Lodge

> *Ten years ago, nine guests vanished from a lodge in the Vermont woods. The case was closed. Tonight, the detectives who closed it have been invited back.*

**Blackwood Lodge** is a social deduction mystery for **6–10 players**. One or two of you are cultists. The rest are investigators. You have **30 minutes** to solve a crime, survive the night, and escape before dawn.

There are **no meetings** and no round resets. The clock does not stop. Talk, search, accuse, lie, and run.

## Quick start

1. **Join a lobby.** The game starts at 6 players; the best experience is 8–10.
2. **Shop.** You have 100 Shillings and four minutes in the Curio Shop. Buy tools that help you investigate.
3. **Arrive.** The storm cuts the road. The host is missing. A guest is dead.
4. **Investigate.** Search rooms, interview staff, and solve the four Case File tasks.
5. **Survive.** The killer can mark you. Break the sigil before the timer runs out.
6. **Ritual.** Complete the four tasks, perform the ritual, and learn the Hollow Word.
7. **Escape.** Reach the gate with the word before 7:00 AM.

## What makes it different

- **No meetings.** There is no emergency button, no discussion circle, and no vote to skip. The match is one continuous night.
- **Evidence, not accusation.** The Case File asks four questions. You need clues, not louder voices.
- **The house changes.** The map is generated every session, and the lodge shifts at midnight.
- **Items change what you know.** A free clue eliminates one option. The right item reveals the truth.
- **Death has a timer.** A marked player can be saved by anyone who solves the sigil puzzle.

## Good to know

- **Free to play.** Cosmetics are optional and never affect gameplay.
- **Voice is recommended, never required.** Every social action works in text.
- **Sessions are short.** One match is about 30 minutes.
- **Spoilers are not welcome.** Please do not post scenario answers, killer identities, or the Hollow Word in public chat or community spaces.
