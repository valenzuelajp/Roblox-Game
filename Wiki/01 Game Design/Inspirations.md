# Inspirations

## Social deduction games

| Game | Borrow | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Among Us** | Clear roles, readable tasks, the joy of a lie | Meeting phases, emergency buttons |
| **Town of Salem** | Role variety, night actions | Complex role lists, spreadsheet meta |
| **Deceit** | Paranoia, trust, a traitor who must act normal | The transformation twist; too telegraphed |
| **Project Winter** | Proximity voice, survival tasks, betrayal | Long crafting, hunger/thirst |
| **Dread Hunger** | Asymmetric roles, sabotage, voice | The survival sim scope |
| **The Ship** | A closed map, a killer among passengers | Clunky combat, dated UI |
| **Goose Goose Duck** | Social fun, accessible roles | Cosmetics-first design |
| **Murder Mystery (Roblox)** | Pick-up-and-play clarity | The round reset; we want one continuous night |
| **Flee the Facility** | Chase tension, escape fantasy | The simple loop; we want deduction |

## Mystery and noir

| Work | Borrow | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| *The Maltese Falcon* | Mood, dialogue, moral fog | Slow pacing |
| *Chinatown* | Layered secrets, a city that lies | Hopeless ending |
| *L.A. Noire* | Evidence reading, interrogation | Expensive facial tech |
| *Knives Out* | A closed circle, a readable crime | Comedic tone |
| *Murder on the Orient Express* | Everyone is a suspect | A single fixed solution |
| *Clue* | Rooms as puzzle pieces | Board-game abstraction |
| *Return of the Obra Dinn* | Deduction from frozen evidence | Monochrome niche appeal |

## Horror and gothic

| Work | Borrow | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| *The Haunting of Hill House* | The house as a character | Pure ghost horror |
| *The Shining* | Isolation, wrong architecture | Iconic imagery |
| *Crimson Peak* | Gothic romance, secrets in the walls | Melodrama |
| *Bloodborne* | Cult iconography, cosmic dread | Gore, difficulty |
| *Resident Evil* (mansion) | Locked doors, keys, room logic | Zombies |
| *Amnesia: The Dark Descent* | Sound, darkness, vulnerability | No combat; we need guns |

## Procedural and systemic

| Work | Borrow | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| *Betrayal at House on the Hill* | Rooms revealed as you explore, a haunt | Randomness without logic |
| *Mansions of Madness* | Modular tiles, scenario variety | Setup time |
| *Clue* | A crime defined by room, weapon, suspect | Fixed board |
| *Return of the Obra Dinn* | A deduction ledger | Slow pacing |

## Roblox-specific

| Game | Borrow | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| *Doors* | Atmospheric lighting, sound, a readable threat | RNG unfairness |
| *Murder Mystery 2* | Role clarity, a simple gun loop | Round reset |
| *Flee the Facility* | Chase music, escape energy | No deduction |
| *Da Hood* | Nothing; the tone is wrong | — |

## The synthesis

Blackwood Lodge is:

- **Among Us** without meetings.
- **Clue** with a procedural house.
- **Project Winter** without the survival grind.
- **L.A. Noire** in a haunted mansion.
- **Betrayal at House on the Hill** as a real-time social game.
