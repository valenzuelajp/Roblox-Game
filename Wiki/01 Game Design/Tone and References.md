# Tone and References

## The sentence

*A hard-boiled detective story trapped inside a haunted house, lit by a dying chandelier and a storm that will not let anyone leave.*

## The three layers

1. **Noir (1940s):** rain, trench coats, typewriters, venetian-blind shadows, old money, cigarettes (unlit, for rating), saxophone, hard-boiled narration.
2. **Gothic mansion:** dark wood, red velvet, antlers, portraits, a locked chapel, a cellar that smells of earth.
3. **Cult horror:** antler sigils, candle circles, whispered Latin, a congregation that was never really gone.

The noir layer is the point of view. The gothic layer is the stage. The cult layer is the threat.

## Visual palette

| Element | Color | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Rain / night | `#0B0F14` | Exterior, windows, shadows |
| Old wood | `#3B2A1E` | Paneling, floors, furniture |
| Velvet | `#6E1F2A` | Curtains, chairs, carpet |
| Brass | `#B08D57` | Fixtures, frames, handles |
| Lamplight | `#E0B25A` | Warm pools of light |
| Moonlight | `#8FA6B2` | Cold rim light |
| Blood (stylized) | `#7A1C1C` | Stains, sigils, props |
| Sigil glow | `#C9A227` | Marked players, ritual |
| Wrongness | `#4B2E5A` | Cult magic, hidden passages |

## Architecture

- A Vermont lodge built on old money, expanded over decades: Queen Anne exterior, Georgian interior, Victorian excess, a chapel that does not match the blueprint.
- Three floors plus a cellar and attic.
- Central staircase; servant stairs; dumbwaiter; coal chute; hidden passages.
- Rooms should feel lived-in but abandoned: dust sheets, half-packed trunks, a dinner that was never finished.
- The house should be readable at a glance but not memorizable: distinct floor themes, landmarks, and sightlines.

## Props and set dressing

- Typewriters, ledgers, letters, telegram slips, guest books, court documents.
- Antler motifs: chandelier, coat rack, bookends, altar.
- Clocks: stopped at 12:00, then running again.
- Mirrors: too many; some covered.
- Portraits: the nine guests, the Blackwood family, a figure with no face.
- Rain: windows, skylights, leaks, wet footprints.

## Costumes

- Investigators: trench coats, fedoras, suits, gloves, scarves, practical shoes.
- Killer: the same silhouette, but one accessory that echoes the cult (an antler pin, a black glove, a signet ring) — never obvious.
- Madame Vesper: layered shawls, rings, a veil, a knowing smile.
- Staff: formal but worn; aprons, keys, white gloves.
- The victim: scenario-specific; the outfit is a clue.

## UI tone

- Case File: manila folder, typewriter labels, red string, brass pins.
- Shop: warm lamplight, handwritten price tags, velvet display.
- Map: blueprint paper, ink annotations, coffee stains.
- Ritual: candlelight, gold sigils, a timer that looks like a burning fuse.
- Results: a newspaper front page with the match headline.

## Audio direction

- Base layer: rain on glass, thunder, wind in the pines, the house settling.
- Music: sparse piano, low strings, a saxophone motif, a choir that only appears near the chapel.
- Stingers: a bell, a clock tick, a sigil hum, a heartbeat, a gunshot that leaves ringing.
- Voice: a hard-boiled narrator for the intro and results; no narrator during play (it would give away information).

## Content boundaries

- Stylized blood, no gore, no dismemberment, no torture.
- No real-world religious symbols used disrespectfully; the cult is fictional.
- No sexual content.
- No gambling, loot boxes, or pay-to-win.
- Respect Roblox's age rating and chat filters.
- Horror is atmosphere and implication, not shock.

## References

| Reference | What to take | What to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| *The Maltese Falcon* | Mood, dialogue rhythm, moral ambiguity | Pacing that is too slow for Roblox |
| *Chinatown* | Noir atmosphere, a city with secrets | The cynical ending; the game needs a winnable path |
| *L.A. Noire* | Evidence reading, interrogation tone | Facial-tech realism; too expensive |
| *Knives Out* | A closed circle of suspects, a readable crime | A comedic tone; keep it dark |
| *The Haunting of Hill House* | A house that is a character | Pure ghost horror; the killers are human |
| *The Shining* | Isolation, repetition, wrong architecture | Iconic imagery that feels copied |
| *Bloodborne* | Cult iconography, cosmic dread | Gore and difficulty |
| *Clue* | Rooms as puzzle pieces, a satisfying accusation | Board-game abstraction |
| *Among Us* | Social deduction clarity, quick reads | Meeting phases |
| *Project Winter* | Survival + social deduction + proximity voice | Long crafting loops |
| *Dread Hunger* | Role asymmetry and paranoia | The survival sim scope |
| *Return of the Obra Dinn* | Deduction from frozen moments | The monochrome art style; too niche |

## What to avoid

- Cartoonish "spooky" music.
- Jump scares as the main horror tool.
- Gore.
- A narrator that explains the mystery.
- Modern slang in the 1940s dialogue.
- A title that sounds generic ("Murder Mystery Mansion").
- A UI that looks like a generic Roblox simulator.
