# Vision

## Elevator pitch

**Blackwood Lodge** is a 6–10 player noir social-deduction game set in a storm-cut mansion that rearranges itself every session. One or two players are cult killers hiding among the investigators. There are no meetings and no safe table talk. The team hunts physical clues, buys the right tools, solves a four-question Case File, and performs a ritual that reveals the killers to everyone — then has to survive the killers' last hunt and escape before dawn.

## Player fantasy

**As an investigator:** "I am a 1940s detective in a house that hates me. I read the room, I find the thing everyone else missed, and I name the killer before the clock runs out."

**As a killer:** "I staged this crime. I know what the house is hiding. I am going to smile, help them search, and pick them off one by one."

**As a dead player:** "I saw who did it. I cannot tell anyone. I can only watch."

## What makes it different

| Feature | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **No meeting phases** | The game never stops for a discussion table. Paranoia happens while people are moving, searching, and lying. |
| **Physical evidence, not a menu** | Clues are props in rooms. The killer's staging choices become the evidence. |
| **Item-gated truth** | Free clues are vague; the right investigator item reveals the definitive answer. The shop is a real decision, not a cosmetic stop. |
| **A house that changes** | The map is procedural every session. Memory alone cannot solve it. |
| **Ritual reveal** | The killers are revealed on purpose. The endgame is a hunt, not a cutscene. |
| **Death is not the end** | Dead players stay in the match as spectators with dead-only chat. |
| **No pay-to-win** | Everything that affects the match is earned in the match. |

## Target audience

- Roblox players who like social deduction, horror, and mystery.
- Players who want a slower, more atmospheric alternative to round-based games.
- Voice-chat groups and friend lobbies.
- Content creators who want a readable 30-minute story per match.
- Age: Roblox's general audience; the tone is noir horror with stylized blood, not gore. Final age rating to be confirmed with Roblox policy.

## Session shape

- **6–10 players.**
- **30 minutes**, one continuous night, 6 PM to 7 AM in-game.
- **Zero downtime:** the shop is the only pre-match stop; the rest is play.
- **One match = one crime.** No campaign required.

## Platforms

- Roblox PC, mobile, and console.
- Spatial voice strongly recommended; text fallback always available.
- Performance target: 60 FPS on mid-range PC, 30+ FPS on mobile.

## Success metrics

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Match completion | > 80% | The 30-minute length must not feel like a trap. |
| Investigator win rate | 40–60% | Neither side should feel doomed. |
| Killer win rate | 40–60% | Same. |
| First-match retention | > 50% | The tutorial and first night must land. |
| Report rate | < 2% of matches | Voice and paranoia need moderation. |
| Average session | 2–3 matches | The loop should invite "one more." |

## Scope guardrails

- One map family: Blackwood Lodge.
- Ten scenario cards at launch.
- 24–28 rooms at launch.
- Five puzzle families.
- No ranked mode, trading, or user-generated maps at launch.
- No mechanic that requires voice.
- No pay-to-win.

## Non-goals

- Not a shooter. Guns are scarce, loud, and dangerous.
- Not a tycoon or progression grind. Progression is cosmetic and knowledge-based.
- Not a horror gore game. The horror is atmosphere, paranoia, and the house.
- Not a meeting simulator. If a feature adds a long discussion phase, it is off-vision.

## The promise

Every match should produce a story the players can retell: the clue nobody searched, the liar who almost got away, the ritual interrupted at the last second, the shot that killed the wrong person, the escape at 6:59 AM.
