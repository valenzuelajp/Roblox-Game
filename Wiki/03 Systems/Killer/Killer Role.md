# Killer Role

## Role card

> **You are the Hand of the Hollow Court.**
> You staged the scene. You know the truth. The house is on your side.
> Mark the investigators. Break their case. Do not let them leave before dawn.

One killer at **6–7 players**. Two partnered killers at **8–10**.

## Assignment

- The server assigns the killer role secretly at match start.
- The killer is always a player, never an NPC.
- The killer is excluded from the first-pistol pool.
- If a killer disconnects before the first mark, the role is reassigned to a living investigator after 30 seconds. If the match is past the first mark, the role is not reassigned; the remaining killer continues alone.
- The killer cannot be a spectator at start.

## What the killer knows

| Knowledge | Detail |
|---|---|
| Scenario card | Full card: victim, motive, weapon, staging, signature, red herring, word |
| True answers | All four Case File answers |
| Task rooms | The rooms each task is themed around |
| Staging room | The room where the body was placed |
| Map seed | The layout after the first minute; secret passages must be found |
| Investigators | Names, roles, and voice chat (if enabled) |

The killer does **not** know:

- The exact clue containers.
- The investigators' private notes.
- The Hollow Word letters the team has earned.
- The ritual's exact timing unless they are in the chapel.

## Abilities

| Ability | Cost | Cooldown | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mark** | Free | Shared (see [[Mark and Cooldown]]) | Marks a living investigator; spawns a sigil; starts a countdown |
| **False clue** | Free | Once per match | Plants one prop supporting a wrong answer |
| **Flicker** | Free | 90s | Kills lights in one wing for 15s |
| **Lock** | Free | 60s | Locks one door for 20s |
| **Whisper** | Free | 45s | Plays a cult whisper near a player |
| **Cold spot** | Free | 60s | Drops temperature in one room for 30s |
| **Claim pistol** | Item | — | Can pick up or claim any pistol like an investigator |

The killer cannot:

- Mark during the first **60 seconds** of the investigation phase.
- Mark a player who is already marked.
- Mark through walls.
- Mark a player in the safe zone (shop, lobby, escape gate after opening).
- Destroy a true clue.
- Read the Case File UI.
- See the investigators' letters.

## Objectives

1. **Protect the staged scene.** The investigators must not solve all four tasks.
2. **Slow the case.** Mislead, sabotage, and force rescues.
3. **Thin the house.** Mark investigators; make the team spend time and items.
4. **Survive the reveal.** After the ritual, win the Last Hunt or reach parity.
5. **Prevent escape until dawn.** The clock is the killer's best ally.

## Win condition

The killers win if any of the following happens:

- **Parity:** living killers ≥ living investigators.
- **Disarmament:** every pistol holder is dead or detained, and no new gun can be claimed.
- **Dawn:** 7:00 AM arrives and no investigator has escaped.

See [[Win Conditions]] for the full matrix.

## Health and combat

- **2 HP.** Each bullet deals 1 damage.
- At **1 HP**, the killer leaves a blood trail.
- At **0 HP**, the killer is downed; they can be detained or finished.
- The killer can use any pistol they claim.
- The killer cannot heal.

## Constraints

- **No instant kill.** Every mark gives the table a puzzle window.
- **No spawn kill.** The first 60 seconds are mark-free.
- **No soft-lock.** The killer cannot destroy the ritual, the UV fallback, or the last true clue.
- **No voice-only play.** Text players must be able to mark, sabotage, and coordinate.
- **No pay-to-win.** Killer power is not purchasable.

## Counterplay

Investigators can:

- Watch who was near the staging room.
- Compare the free clue and item clue.
- Notice who pushes a wrong answer.
- Guard the sigil.
- Use the UV torch to reveal blood trails.
- Detain the killer at 1 HP.
- Use the ritual to reveal them.

## Roleplay

The killer is a guest. They should have a reason to be at the lodge:

- A journalist covering the anniversary.
- A lawyer representing the victim's estate.
- A relative of one of the nine guests.
- A doctor attending the host.
- A detective who never closed the case.

The scenario card supplies a suggested cover story. The killer is not required to use it.

## Edge cases

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| Killer disconnects before first mark | Role reassigned after 30s |
| Killer disconnects after first mark | Match continues; partner or investigators can still win |
| Both killers disconnect | Investigators win by default after 60s |
| Killer is marked | Killers cannot be marked |
| Killer is detained | See [[Detain System]] |
| Killer claims the second pistol | Allowed; the cabinet does not check role |
| Killer completes the ritual | Allowed; reveal still happens |
