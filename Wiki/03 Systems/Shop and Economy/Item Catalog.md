# Item Catalog

> [!info] Status
> Prices, charges, and effects are **proposed** unless marked locked. The ten investigator items and five killer items are the launch target. The catalog is intentionally small: every item must have a clear job.

## Investigator items

| Item | Price | Charges | Primary job | Task |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | 25 | 3 | Photograph a scene; the flash reveals hidden ink, plaques, and reflections | Victim / Scene |
| **Notebook** | 20 | ∞ | Record testimony and cross-reference names; one hint per task | Victim / Motive |
| **Magnifying Glass** | 25 | ∞ | Examine wounds, fibers, and tool marks; 1s search | Method |
| **Fingerprint Kit** | 30 | 2 | Lift prints from objects and match them to a guest | Method / Scene |
| **UV Torch** | 30 | 4 | Reveal blood, sigils, and hidden writing | Scene |
| **Compass** | 20 | ∞ | Points to the nearest anchor (chapel, vault, exit); orients the map | Utility |
| **Cipher Wheel** | 30 | ∞ | Decode ciphers, zodiac, and binary inscriptions | Motive / Puzzles |
| **Recorder** | 35 | 3 | Record and replay testimony, audio, and the victim's last message | Motive |
| **Lockpick** | 25 | 2 | Open a locked door or container without a key | Utility |
| **Map** | 25 | ∞ | Reveals the current floor layout and marked anchors; updates at midnight | Utility |

### Camera

**Fantasy:** The flashbulb catches what the eye misses.

- **Use:** Aim at a clue, portrait, document, or stain. The photo is added to the team's evidence board.
- **Reveal:** Hidden ink, a scratched-out name, a reflection in a mirror, a plaque under a portrait.
- **Limit:** 3 charges. The flash is visible and audible; the killer can see it from another room.
- **Counter:** The killer can turn off the lights in the room (flicker sabotage) to waste a charge.
- **Task synergy:** Victim (portrait plaque), Scene (reflection in the ballroom mirror).

### Notebook

**Fantasy:** The detective's memory, written down.

- **Use:** Record a testimony line or a clue description. The entry is shared with the team.
- **Reveal:** Cross-referencing two names can eliminate one wrong option. Once per task.
- **Limit:** Unlimited, but each task can only be hinted once.
- **Counter:** The killer can plant a false testimony; the notebook records it as spoken, not as true.
- **Task synergy:** Victim (guest list), Motive (ledger names).

### Magnifying Glass

**Fantasy:** The detail that changes the case.

- **Use:** Examine a wound, a tool, a fiber, or a lock. Search time drops from 2s to 1s.
- **Reveal:** The wound's shape, the tool's missing tooth, the paint chip. This is the definitive Method clue.
- **Limit:** Unlimited.
- **Counter:** The killer can stage a wound that matches the wrong weapon; the glass reveals the mismatch, not the killer.
- **Task synergy:** Method.

### Fingerprint Kit

**Fantasy:** Everyone leaves a trace.

- **Use:** Dust a surface, lift a print, compare it to a guest's print card.
- **Reveal:** Who touched the weapon, the ledger, or the chapel door.
- **Limit:** 2 charges.
- **Counter:** The killer wears gloves (black-market item) or wipes the surface.
- **Task synergy:** Method, Scene.

### UV Torch

**Fantasy:** The room has a second skin.

- **Use:** Sweep a room. Blood, sigils, and hidden writing glow.
- **Reveal:** The blood trail, the ritual sigil under the rug, the message on the wall.
- **Limit:** 4 charges.
- **Counter:** The killer can mask a sigil with paint or bleach (one-time sabotage).
- **Task synergy:** Scene, Hollow Word fallback.

### Compass

**Fantasy:** The house moves; the needle does not.

- **Use:** Points to the nearest **anchor** (chapel, vault, exit), not to clues.
- **Reveal:** Orientation in a shifting map; the direction of the exit.
- **Limit:** Unlimited.
- **Counter:** The killer can rotate the compass for 20 seconds with a sabotage.
- **Task synergy:** Utility, escape.

### Cipher Wheel

**Fantasy:** The code was never meant to be secret. It was meant to be earned.

- **Use:** Decode a cipher, zodiac inscription, or binary message.
- **Reveal:** The decoded text; the Motive letter; a puzzle hint.
- **Limit:** Unlimited.
- **Counter:** The killer can plant a false cipher that decodes to a red herring.
- **Task synergy:** Motive, puzzles, second-pistol seals.

### Recorder

**Fantasy:** The dead get one more sentence.

- **Use:** Record a testimony or an ambient sound. Replay it anywhere.
- **Reveal:** The victim's last message; a staff member's contradiction; a hidden whisper.
- **Limit:** 3 charges.
- **Counter:** The killer can whisper over the recording or destroy the tape (one-time sabotage).
- **Task synergy:** Motive.

### Lockpick

**Fantasy:** Every lock is a suggestion.

- **Use:** Open a locked door or container without the key.
- **Reveal:** A shortcut, a hidden room, a locked chest.
- **Limit:** 2 charges. Some locks are unpickable (chapel main door, vault cabinet).
- **Counter:** The killer can jam a lock for 60 seconds.
- **Task synergy:** Utility, secret passages.

### Map

**Fantasy:** The house is a puzzle; the map is the box lid.

- **Use:** Opens a floor-plan overlay. Shows the current layout and marked anchors.
- **Reveal:** The route to the chapel, vault, and exit. Does **not** show clues or the killer.
- **Limit:** Unlimited. Updates at midnight.
- **Counter:** The killer can steal the map if the holder dies; the map does not show secret passages until discovered.
- **Task synergy:** Utility, midnight shift.

## Killer black-market items

The killer sees a hidden **Black Market** tab in the Curio Shop. It uses the same 100 Shillings. The killer can buy investigator items too, to blend in.

| Item | Price | Charges | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forged Letter** | 30 | 1 | Enables the one false clue. The letter supports a wrong option and looks authentic. |
| **Gloves** | 25 | 1 match | No fingerprints, no blood trail for 60 seconds after a mark. |
| **Wire Cutters** | 20 | 1 | Cut the lights in one wing for 15 seconds, or jam one lock for 60 seconds. |
| **Cult Sigil** | 35 | 1 | Reduce the next mark cooldown by 10 seconds. |
| **Whisper Mask** | 30 | 2 | Play a whisper in a room to bait a player; the whisper sounds like a staff member. |

### Black-market rules

- The killer can buy at most **two** black-market items.
- Black-market items are not visible on the team purchase board.
- The killer cannot buy a pistol, a bullet, or a clue answer.
- The killer can use investigator items normally.
- The black market closes when the shop closes.

## Item counters

Every item has a counter, and every counter has a cost:

| Item | Counter | Counter cost |
|---|---|---|
| Camera | Flicker (lights off) | Sabotage cooldown |
| Notebook | False testimony | One false clue |
| Magnifying Glass | Staged wound | Staging time |
| Fingerprint Kit | Gloves | One black-market purchase |
| UV Torch | Bleach/paint | One sabotage |
| Compass | Rotate needle | One sabotage |
| Cipher Wheel | False cipher | One false clue |
| Recorder | Whisper over tape | One sabotage |
| Lockpick | Jam lock | One sabotage |
| Map | Steal on death | Requires a kill |

## Accessibility

- Every item has a text description and a text clue card.
- The camera's photo is described in text.
- The UV torch's glow has a pattern, not just a color.
- The recorder has subtitles.
- The compass has a directional text readout.
- The map has a text route list.

## Design notes

- **Ten items is the launch target.** More items dilute the clue pairs.
- **Every item must be useful in at least two situations.** A one-note item is a trap.
- **The killer's items must not be strictly better.** They create pressure, not an automatic win.
- **The best item is the one that makes the player ask a better question.**
