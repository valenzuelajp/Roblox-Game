# Second Pistol

## The race

The second pistol is the **mid-game objective**. It is not awarded; it is **claimed**.

Three seals are hidden across the three floors:

| Seal | Floor | Family | Location examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Seal I** | Ground | Zodiac | Study, ballroom, chapel door |
| **Seal II** | Second | Binary | Archive, fuse box, music room |
| **Seal III** | Third | Knot/pattern or rune | Attic, tapestry, bell tower |

Each seal is a **hard** puzzle. Solving a seal locks it permanently for the match. Solving all three opens the **vault cabinet** in the cellar vault. The first player to interact with the cabinet claims the pistol.

## The cabinet

- Located in the **vault** (cellar level).
- Sealed until all three seals are solved.
- The cabinet glows faintly when ready.
- The first interaction starts a **2-second claim**.
- The claim is interrupted by damage.
- The claimant can be an investigator **or a killer**.
- The pistol has **four bullets**.

## Why the killer can claim it

The second pistol is a **race**, not a reward. If the investigators are slow, the killer gets a ranged weapon and the endgame tilts. This is intentional:

- It gives the killer a reason to leave the shadows.
- It gives the investigators a reason to split up.
- It creates the "who got the gun?" moment.

## The seals

Each seal is a different puzzle family:

1. **Seal I — Zodiac.** A brass wheel with a missing sign. The answer is the victim's birth sign.
2. **Seal II — Binary.** A fuse box with eight switches. The answer is the pattern of lit windows.
3. **Seal III — Knot/pattern.** A tapestry with a missing repeat. The answer is the branch count.

The seals are **not** in the same room. The team must traverse all three floors. The midnight shift can change the route to one seal.

## The Ghost Blueprint

The **Ghost Blueprint** (Task 4 reward) shows the original layout and the three seal locations. Without it, the team must find the seals by exploration. With it, they can plan the race.

## Claiming rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Claim time | 2 seconds |
| Interrupt | Damage cancels the claim |
| Contest | Two players cannot claim at once; the first interaction wins |
| Killer claim | Allowed |
| Drop | The pistol drops on death |
| Ammo | 4 |
| Reload | None |

## Counterplay

Investigators can:

- Split into three teams to solve the seals in parallel.
- Guard the vault after the third seal.
- Use the Ghost Blueprint to find the seals.
- Let the killer solve a seal, then ambush them at the cabinet.

The killer can:

- Solve a seal to learn its location.
- Camp the vault.
- Claim the pistol first.
- Destroy the cabinet? No — the cabinet cannot be destroyed.

## Presentation

- **Seal solved:** a deep chime, a line of light across the floor.
- **All three solved:** the vault door clicks; the cabinet glows.
- **Claim:** a brass handle turns; the pistol is lifted; the lights flicker.
- **Killer claim:** the same animation, but the music drops out.

## Edge cases

| Case | Rule |
|---|---|
| All three seals solved but no one claims | The cabinet stays open until claimed |
| Claimant dies during claim | The claim cancels; the gun remains |
| Claimant is marked during claim | The mark does not cancel; damage does |
| Killer claims and dies | The gun drops; anyone can pick it up |
| Match ends before claim | The gun is not awarded |

## Design notes

- The second pistol is the **mid-game spike**. It should arrive around 18–24 minutes.
- Three seals is the right number: enough to force traversal, not enough to feel like a fetch quest.
- The killer's ability to claim is the twist. It makes the race personal.
- The vault should be a **dangerous room**: one entrance, dark, echoey, easy to camp.
