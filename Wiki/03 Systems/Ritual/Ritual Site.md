# Ritual Site

## Why the chapel

The chapel is the ritual site because it is:

- **Thematic.** The cult's oldest room, built over the Hollow.
- **Readable.** Two entrances, a central altar, a bell rope, and a stained-glass window.
- **Defensible.** Investigators can guard both doors, but the killer can enter through the crypt.
- **Fair.** No single chokepoint; no safe camping spot.

The chapel is **sealed until the midnight shift**. Before midnight, the doors are locked and the stained glass is dark. At midnight, the bell rings once and the doors open.

## Layout

```text
                 [stained glass]
        [side door]   [altar]   [bell rope]
             |          |           |
        [pews] ---- [aisle] ---- [pews]
             |          |           |
        [main door]  [crypt stair] [storage]
```

- **Main door:** foyer side; wide; visible from the aisle.
- **Side door:** garden side; narrow; the killer's preferred entrance.
- **Crypt stair:** cellar; locked until midnight; the killer can use it.
- **Storage:** small room with candles, rope, and a spare bell rope.

## The altar

The altar is a stone table with five candle holders. It is the channel point.

- The channeler stands behind it, facing the stained glass.
- The five candles light in sequence.
- The fifth candle is black; it lights last.
- The altar has a hidden compartment (UV fallback letter).

## The anchors

| Anchor | Location | Interaction | Killer counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Word** | Altar | Channeler holds 30s | Mark the channeler |
| **Blood** | Floor sigil | Second player stands on it | Break the sigil |
| **Bell** | Bell rope | Third player rings at start | Cut the rope (one-time sabotage) |

The anchors are visible from the altar. The channeler can see whether they are active.

## Lighting

- **Before midnight:** cold moonlight through the stained glass; candles unlit.
- **During the ritual:** five candles, warm; the fifth is red.
- **After the reveal:** the stained glass glows from behind; the chapel is the brightest room in the house.
- **Blackout:** the candles are the only light; the UV fallback is visible under the altar.

## Sound

- **Rain** on the roof, louder than anywhere else.
- **Bell** once at midnight and once at the reveal.
- **Choir** a low, wordless hum during the channel; it stops when the channel breaks.
- **Footsteps** on stone; the crypt stair echoes.

## Killer counterplay

The killer can:

- Enter through the crypt stair.
- Cut the bell rope once.
- Break the blood sigil.
- Mark the channeler.
- Wait outside the main door and mark a guard.
- Use the Last Hunt to rush the altar after the reveal.

The killer cannot:

- Lock the chapel doors permanently.
- Destroy the altar.
- Stop the UV fallback.

## Investigator counterplay

Investigators can:

- Guard both doors.
- Put a pistol holder at the side door.
- Keep a second player on the blood sigil.
- Ring the bell early to bait the killer.
- Use the UV torch to find the fallback letter if the ritual is blocked.

## Accessibility

- The chapel has no stairs between the main door and the altar.
- The crypt stair has a handrail and a text prompt.
- The candles have a high-contrast mode.
- The bell rope has a click interaction, not a motion gesture.

## Art notes

- **Stained glass:** the nine guests, plus one empty pane. The empty pane is the tenth guest.
- **Pews:** ten rows, but only nine have hymnals.
- **Altar:** antler carving, five candle holders, a black fifth candle.
- **Floor sigil:** a hollow circle with a five-letter ring; the letters are worn away.
- **Bell:** cracked; the crack is new.
