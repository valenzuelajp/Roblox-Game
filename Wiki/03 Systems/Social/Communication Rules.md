# Communication Rules

> [!info] Locked frame
> There are **no meeting phases** (D-003). Communication is open mic and proximity text. There is no discussion table, no round reset, and no global voice channel for investigators.

## The social contract

Blackwood Lodge is a game about **trust under pressure**. The rules exist to keep the pressure fair:

1. **Talk is proximity-based.** You can only hear/read players near you.
2. **The dead cannot talk to the living.** See [[Dead Chat]].
3. **The killer can lie.** Lying is the game.
4. **The killer cannot leak the scenario.** The killer knows the answers; saying them out loud is allowed but suspicious.
5. **No outside information.** Discord calls, stream sniping, and second screens are cheating.
6. **No harassment.** Deception is not abuse.
7. **No spoilers.** Do not tell new players the story or the scenario.
8. **No ghosting.** A dead player cannot guide the living through voice, text, or external chat.

## What is allowed

- Lying about your role.
- Lying about evidence.
- Accusing someone without proof.
- Refusing to share a clue.
- Trading items.
- Forming temporary alliances.
- Refusing to vote.
- Roleplaying a noir detective.

## What is not allowed

- **Metagaming:** using information from outside the game (stream, Discord, a friend in the lobby).
- **Stream sniping:** watching a stream to find the killer.
- **Ghost communication:** a dead player telling the living what they see.
- **Voice leaks:** a dead player in the same voice call as a living player.
- **Exploiting:** speed hacks, ESP, teleporting, or reading the server.
- **Harassment:** slurs, threats, doxxing, sexual content, or targeted abuse.
- **Spoiling:** revealing the scenario, the killer's identity, or the ending to new players.
- **Griefing:** intentionally ruining the match for your team (e.g., shooting an innocent on purpose, blocking the ritual for no reason).

## Accusation without meetings

There is no trial. Accusation is a **social act**:

- Say it in proximity voice.
- Type it in case chat.
- Pin it to the evidence board.
- Ping the suspect.
- Vote when the vote window opens.

The accused can respond, ignore, or lie. The team can believe, doubt, or split. The game does not resolve the argument; the players do.

## The evidence board as a communication tool

The board is the closest thing to a shared notebook:

- **Pins:** a clue, a photo, a name.
- **Strings:** a connection between two pins.
- **Notes:** a short text note.
- **Suspects:** a name with a confidence marker.
- **Letters:** the Hollow Word bank.

The board is visible to all living players. The killer can tamper with it once per match (move a pin, hide a photo, add a false card).

## Voice etiquette

- **Don't talk over the dead.** They cannot answer.
- **Don't shout.** The game's audio is designed for quiet tension.
- **Don't stream-snipe.** If you are streaming, use a delay.
- **Don't pressure minors to use voice.** Text is always enough.
- **Don't use voice changers** to impersonate another player. Cosmetic voice changers are allowed only if they are clearly labeled.

## Text etiquette

- Keep case chat to the case.
- Use pings for location, not spam.
- Do not flood the board with notes.
- Do not use all-caps.
- Do not share personal information.

## Reporting

The report flow:

1. Open the player list.
2. Select the player.
3. Choose a reason: harassment, cheating, voice abuse, chat abuse, griefing, other.
4. Add a short description.
5. Submit.

Reports are logged with the match seed and the game events. Voice is not recorded; text is.

## Penalties

| Behavior | First offense | Repeat |
|---|---|---|
| Metagaming | Warning | Match ban |
| Ghost communication | Warning | Match ban |
| Harassment | Match ban | Account report |
| Cheating | Match ban + report | Permanent ban |
| Griefing | Warning | Match ban |
| Spoiling | Warning | Chat mute |

Penalties are applied by the game's moderation system and Roblox's moderation. The design team does not moderate individual matches.

## Design notes

- **The rules should be short enough to read in the lobby.**
- **The game should not rely on players policing each other.** The report flow must be one click.
- **Deception is not toxicity.** The rules must protect liars and detectives equally.
- **The best social game makes the players want to be fair.**
