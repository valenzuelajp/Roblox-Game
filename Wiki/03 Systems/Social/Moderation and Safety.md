# Moderation and Safety

> [!info] Status
> This page is a **proposed** safety plan. Roblox's platform rules are mandatory; the game-specific rules are recommendations.

## The safety principle

A social deduction game asks players to lie, accuse, and distrust. That is fun only if everyone feels **safe enough to play**. Safety is not the opposite of paranoia; it is the floor that makes paranoia possible.

## Platform rules

Blackwood Lodge must comply with Roblox's:

- Community Standards.
- Voice chat rules.
- Chat filter.
- Age and privacy settings.
- Advertising and monetization rules.
- Data privacy rules.

The game cannot override Roblox's filters or age gates. It can add stricter rules.

## The four safety layers

1. **Platform:** Roblox moderation, chat filter, voice verification, reporting.
2. **Game:** report button, mute, block, kick, match ban, seed logging.
3. **Design:** no gore, no sexual content, no real-world hate symbols, no harm to minors.
4. **Community:** lobby etiquette, code of conduct, creator communication.

## Reporting

The in-game report flow:

- Player list → select player → reason → description → submit.
- Reasons: harassment, hate speech, sexual content, cheating, griefing, voice abuse, chat abuse, spoilers, other.
- The report includes the match seed, the timestamp, the player's role (investigator/killer), and the last 60 seconds of text/game events.
- Voice is **not recorded**. Roblox handles voice reports.
- The reporter receives a confirmation, not the outcome.

## Blocking and muting

- **Mute:** hides a player's voice and text for the rest of the match.
- **Block:** prevents matchmaking with the player in future sessions (platform permitting).
- **Kill switch:** a player can leave the match at any time; the match continues.
- **Spectator:** a player who leaves can spectate, but cannot communicate with the living.

## Age and privacy

- The game does not ask for real names, ages, locations, or contact details.
- The game does not display Roblox display names in the player wiki or external systems.
- Voice chat is opt-in and age-gated by Roblox.
- Minors are protected by Roblox's default settings.
- The game does not use external voice servers.

## Content boundaries

| Content | Allowed? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stylized blood | Yes | Dark stains, ribbons, smears; no gore |
| Death | Yes | Implied, theatrical; no graphic violence |
| Cult imagery | Yes | Fictional antlers, knots, hollow circle |
| Real-world hate symbols | **No** | Zero tolerance |
| Sexual content | **No** | The tone is adult, the content is not |
| Harm to children | **No** | The child's toy is a ghost story, not a depiction |
| Self-harm | **No** | Not a theme |
| Real-world religions | **No** | The cult is fictional |
| Alcohol/tobacco | Yes, period-appropriate | No glamorized use by minors |
| Jump scares | Optional | Accessibility toggle |

## The killer's experience

Being the killer is stressful. The game should:

- Tell the killer clearly what is expected.
- Give them a private channel with their partner.
- Remind them that lying is the role, not a personal failing.
- Protect them from post-match harassment.
- Let them leave without penalty if they are uncomfortable.

## The victim's experience

Being killed early is frustrating. The game should:

- Give the dead player a meaningful spectator mode.
- Let them watch the rest of the match.
- Give them dead chat.
- Award Cinders for the time played.
- Never mock the dead player in the UI.

## The new player's experience

- The lobby has a 60-second tutorial.
- The first match is against other new players if possible.
- The game does not put a new player in a 10-player killer lobby.
- The player can spectate a match before playing.
- The player can disable voice and still complete the tutorial.

## Crisis and harassment

If a player is in crisis or being harassed:

- The report button is always available.
- The game shows a "leave match" button.
- The lobby links to Roblox's safety resources.
- The game does not encourage contact outside Roblox.

## Moderation tooling

The backend should provide:

- Match seed lookup.
- Text chat logs.
- Game event logs (marks, votes, shots, ritual).
- Report queue.
- Ban and mute tools.
- False-report detection.
- Appeal flow.

## Design notes

- **Safety is a feature, not a compliance checkbox.**
- **The report flow must be faster than the harassment.**
- **The game should never punish a player for leaving.**
- **The killer should be able to say "I need a break" without losing rewards.**
- **The dead should have something to do.**
