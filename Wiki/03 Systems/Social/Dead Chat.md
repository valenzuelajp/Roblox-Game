# Dead Chat

> [!info] Proposed default
> Dead chat is **text only**, visible to all dead players, invisible to the living. It is the recommended part of O-001 Option A.

## Why text only?

Voice dead chat is a moderation and fairness hazard:

- A dead player in the same voice call as a living friend can leak information.
- Roblox spatial voice does not have a "dead-only" channel that is guaranteed separate.
- A text channel is easy to log, filter, and moderate.
- A text channel keeps the dead from shouting over the living.

The dead can still talk to each other. They just have to type.

## What the dead can share

- What they saw before they died.
- Who they suspect.
- Where the killer was.
- What the living are doing.
- Jokes, reactions, and commentary.

The living cannot see any of it.

## What the dead cannot do

- Speak to the living.
- Ping the living.
- Vote.
- Open doors.
- Search containers.
- Pick up items.
- Tamper with the evidence board.
- Enter the killer's private channel.
- Share external links.
- Harass the living through a second account or external voice.

## The dead chat UI

- A small text panel in the corner of the spectator UI.
- The panel is collapsed by default; the player can expand it.
- Messages show the dead player's display name and the time.
- The chat is filtered by Roblox's chat filter.
- The chat has a report button.
- The chat is disabled for players who leave the match.

## The social purpose

Dead chat is not just a consolation prize. It is a **second game**:

- The dead compare notes.
- The dead argue about the case.
- The dead watch the killer and comment.
- The dead can root for the living without helping them.
- The dead can warn each other about a toxic player.

The best dead chat feels like the **press box** at a masquerade.

## Moderation

- Dead chat is logged.
- Reports from dead chat are prioritized.
- A dead player who leaks information to the living is subject to the same penalties as a living player.
- A dead player who harasses the living through external voice is reported to Roblox.

## Accessibility

- Text-to-speech for dead chat.
- Adjustable text size.
- High-contrast mode.
- The chat can be hidden entirely.
- The chat does not auto-scroll if the player is reading.

## Design notes

- **The dead should not be silenced, only separated.**
- **Text-only is a feature, not a limitation.** It keeps the living game clean.
- **The dead chat should be fun enough that players stay to the end.**
- **Never show the killer's identity in dead chat before the ritual.** The dead can speculate, not confirm.
