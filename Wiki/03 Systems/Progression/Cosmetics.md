# Cosmetics

> [!info] Expression, not advantage
> Cosmetics are how a player says "I was there" or "I solved that." They must never make a player harder to see, easier to read, or more powerful.

## Categories

| Category | Examples | Slot |
|---|---|---|
| **Outfit** | Trench coat, evening gown, servant's uniform, fisherman's sweater | Body |
| **Hat** | Fedora, veil, cap, deerstalker, none | Head |
| **Glasses** | Round spectacles, sunglasses, monocle, cracked lens | Face |
| **Gloves** | Leather, silk, work gloves, bloodstained cuff | Hands |
| **Gun skin** | Engraved silver, blackwood, ivory grip, rusted | Pistol |
| **Lobby theme** | Rain on glass, fireplace, chapel, archive, empty ballroom | Lobby |
| **Emote** | Tip hat, check watch, light pipe, shrug, knock on wood | Social |
| **Title** | "The Whole Truth," "Ninth Guest," "Cage Keeper" | Nameplate |
| **Casebook skin** | Leather, ash, cyanotype, red string | UI |

## Art rules

The tone is **1940s noir**: wool, silk, leather, brass, rain, cigarette smoke (stylized), old money, worn shoes. Avoid:

- Neon, cyberpunk, or sci-fi elements.
- Cartoonish oversized weapons.
- Gore or realistic wounds.
- Real-world hate symbols.
- Sexualized outfits.
- Anything that breaks the silhouette fairness rule.

## Fairness constraints

Cosmetics can change **color and detail**, not **readability**:

- No pure-black outfit that disappears in a blackout.
- No pure-white outfit that glows in moonlight.
- No outfit that changes the player's hitbox or collision.
- No hat that hides the nameplate or voice indicator.
- No gun skin that changes the muzzle flash color in a way that mimics a signal.
- No emote that looks like a gameplay action (e.g., a fake mark or fake surrender).
- No cosmetic that only the killer can see.

All cosmetics are validated against a **silhouette test**: at 30 studs, in rain, in blackout, the player must be recognizable as a player.

## Rarity and pricing

| Rarity | Price (Robux) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Common | 49–99 | Rank, Cinders, direct |
| Rare | 149–249 | Direct, badges |
| Legendary | 399–599 | Direct, mastery |
| Bundle | 699–999 | Direct, seasonal |

Prices are proposed. There are **no loot boxes**, no keys, no random cosmetic drops, and no paid currency that can be converted into gameplay.

## Sets and bundles

A set is a matched outfit, hat, glasses, and gun skin. Bundles are direct purchases with a visible contents list. No "mystery bundle." No "get a random legendary."

## Seasonal content

Seasonal sets are allowed:

- Autumn: harvest, hunting, rain.
- Winter: snow, fur, chapel candles.
- Spring: mud, flowers, reopening the lodge.
- Summer: heat, fireflies, the road out.

Seasonal items are **direct purchase** or **event reward**. They do not expire from the player's inventory. A missed season is not a punishment.

## User-generated content

No UGC at launch. If UGC is added later:

- It must pass the same silhouette and content rules.
- It must be moderated.
- It must not be sold for gameplay advantage.
- It must not include real-world hate symbols or sexual content.
