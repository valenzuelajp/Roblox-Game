# Badges

> [!important] Anti-toxicity rule
> There is no badge for killing investigators, betraying teammates, or winning by deception. Badges reward **playing the case**, not hurting people.

## Match badges

| Badge | Criteria | Display |
|---|---|---|
| **First on the Scene** | Find the first free clue of the match | Nameplate flash |
| **Letter by Letter** | Solve a task without the item clue | Casebook stamp |
| **Under the UV** | Find an item clue with the UV Torch | Profile icon |
| **Sigil Breaker** | Break three sigils in one match | Emote unlock |
| **The Long Night** | Survive to dawn as an investigator | Title |
| **The Whole Truth** | 4/4 tasks, ritual, both killers stopped, escape | Gold casebook stamp |
| **Open Case** | Stop the killers with an incomplete case | Silver stamp |
| **The Cage** | Detain both killers and escape | Title |
| **House Keeps Its Own** | Stop both killers but fail to escape | Ash stamp |
| **Innocent** | Shoot an innocent investigator | Mark on record (cosmetic only) |
| **Ninth Rite** | Win as a killer at dawn | Cult cosmetic |
| **Tenth Guest** | Eliminate every investigator | Cult cosmetic |

## Story badges

| Badge | Criteria | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **The Nine** | Read all nine guest entries in the casebook | Lore collection |
| **The Letter** | Find Vesper's letter in the archive | Lore |
| **The Blueprint** | Use the Ghost Blueprint to open the cellar ritual door | Lore |
| **The Word** | Discover all ten Hollow Words | Long-term collection |
| **Master Detective** | Master all ten scenarios | Prestige |

## Challenge badges

| Badge | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **No Items, No Problem** | Solve 4/4 tasks without buying an investigator item |
| **Text Only** | Complete a match without voice chat |
| **Blackout** | Solve a task during a blackout |
| **Pacifist** | Win without firing a pistol |
| **Speedrun** | Complete the ritual before 20:00 |
| **One Bullet** | Stop a killer with the last bullet in the gun |

Challenge badges are optional. They should never encourage throwing or griefing.

## Display rules

- Badges appear on the player's profile and in the end-of-match ledger.
- A player can choose one badge to show on their nameplate.
- Badges are permanent. They are not seasonal.
- Badges do not grant gameplay power.
- Badges can grant cosmetics or titles, but those are also cosmetic.

## Roblox integration

Use Roblox's badge system for platform-level badges where possible. In-game badges can mirror them. Do not use paid badges.
