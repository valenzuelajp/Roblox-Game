# Progression Overview

> [!info] Two tracks
> **Match progression** is what happens in the 30 minutes: tasks, letters, pistols, survival. **Account progression** is what persists: the casebook, Cinders, badges, cosmetics, and titles. The two never sell power to each other.

## Principles

1. **No pay-to-win.** Nothing purchasable changes clues, bullets, health, speed, or information.
2. **Earned, not ground.** Progression should reward playing well and playing often, not grinding a currency faucet.
3. **The casebook is the trophy.** The main long-term collection is the scenarios you have solved and the words you have discovered.
4. **Cosmetics are the expression.** Outfits, hats, glasses, gloves, gun skins, lobby themes, and titles.
5. **No dark patterns.** No loot boxes, no paid random rewards, no limited-time gambling, no FOMO that punishes a missed week.
6. **Spoilers are progression.** A scenario's casebook page unlocks only after the player has played it, and the true answers unlock only after the case is solved.

## Match progression

| Beat | What the player earns | Persistence |
|---|---|---|
| First task solved | 1 letter, Cinders, casebook clue | Cinders and casebook entry persist |
| First pistol | Combat agency | Match only |
| Second pistol | Combat agency | Match only |
| Ritual completed | Fifth letter, killer reveal, exit unlock | Match only |
| Killer stopped | 100% or 50% payout tier | Cinders and badge progress persist |
| Escape | Win state | Badge progress persists |
| Death | Spectator; partial Cinders | Cinders persist |

## Account progression

### The Casebook

The casebook is a leather-bound collection of every scenario the player has encountered:

- **Unsolved:** the card's title, victim silhouette, and a redacted summary.
- **Solved:** the full card, the true answers, the Hollow Word, the staging room, and a short epilogue.
- **Mastered:** solved with 4/4 tasks and the killer stopped. Adds a gold stamp.

The casebook is the primary collection. It gives players a reason to replay scenarios and to try the harder cards.

### Detective Rank

A proposed account level based on Cinders earned and cases solved. Rank unlocks:

- Cosmetic slots (a second outfit preset, a title slot).
- Casebook skins.
- Lobby themes.
- Emotes.

Rank does **not** unlock items, bullets, clues, or map advantages. Rank is a measure of experience, not power.

### Badges and titles

Badges are permanent achievements. Titles are display-only strings. See [[Badges]].

## Progression pacing

| Player stage | Goal | Target |
|---|---|---|
| First match | Understand the loop, earn first Cinders, unlock one cosmetic | 30 minutes |
| First 5 matches | Solve a case, see the ritual, earn a badge | 2–3 hours |
| First 20 matches | Complete the casebook's first page, try a hard scenario | 8–10 hours |
| Long term | Master every scenario, collect cosmetic sets, earn titles | Ongoing |

The first match should always end with **something** unlocked, even if the player loses. A loss still teaches the loop and pays Cinders.

## What is not progression

- No power upgrades.
- No paid clue packs.
- No bullet purchases.
- No "premium killer" role.
- No map packs that split the queue.
- No loot boxes.
- No battle pass that expires the story.

## Telemetry

Track:

- Matches per player per week.
- Casebook completion rate.
- Cinders earned and spent.
- Cosmetic conversion.
- Rank distribution.
- First-match retention.
- Badge earn rates.
- Refund and report rates.
