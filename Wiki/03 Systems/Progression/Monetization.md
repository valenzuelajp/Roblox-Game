# Monetization

> [!important] Locked direction
> **Cosmetic-only. No pay-to-win. No loot boxes. No gameplay advantage for sale.** This is a design pillar and a Roblox policy decision.

## Revenue streams

| Stream | What is sold | Why it is fair |
|---|---|---|
| **Cosmetics** | Outfits, hats, glasses, gloves, gun skins, lobby themes, emotes | Visual only; no gameplay effect |
| **Private servers** | A private lobby for friends | Social convenience; no gameplay advantage |
| **Casebook skins** | UI themes for the casebook | Visual only |
| **Seasonal sets** | Direct-purchase themed cosmetics | No randomness; no expiry |
| **Roblox Premium payouts** | Platform engagement payout | Platform revenue, not player power |

## What is never sold

- Investigator items.
- Extra bullets.
- Extra health.
- Extra speed.
- Clue locations.
- Killer identity.
- Scenario answers.
- A "premium killer" role.
- A map that only paying players see.
- A vote advantage.
- A revive.

## Pricing philosophy

- Common cosmetics: 49–99 Robux.
- Rare: 149–249 Robux.
- Legendary: 399–599 Robux.
- Bundles: 699–999 Robux.
- Private server: 50–100 Robux per month (proposed).

Prices should be regionalized by Roblox. No fake discounts, no countdown pressure, no "buy before you lose it" unless it is a genuine seasonal event with a clear end date.

## Roblox policy compliance

- Follow Roblox's cosmetic and UGC rules.
- No gambling mechanics.
- No paid random rewards.
- No real-world currency trading.
- No external payment links.
- No misleading thumbnails.
- No age-inappropriate content.
- No pay-to-win in competitive experiences.

## Trust rules

1. **Show the contents.** A bundle lists every item before purchase.
2. **No mystery boxes.** Ever.
3. **No pay-to-skip.** You cannot buy a task answer.
4. **No pay-to-revive.** Death is part of the game.
5. **No pay-to-voice.** Voice is free or unavailable; never sold.
6. **Refunds follow Roblox policy.** Do not promise more than the platform allows.
7. **No dark patterns.** No fake scarcity, no confusing currency conversion, no hidden recurring charges.
8. **No spoilers in the store.** Cosmetic names and descriptions must not reveal scenario answers.

## Metrics

- Cosmetic conversion rate.
- Average revenue per paying user.
- Private server retention.
- Refund rate.
- Report rate tied to purchases.
- Player sentiment on fairness.
