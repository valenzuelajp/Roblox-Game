# Knot and Pattern

## The idea

The cult binds, weaves, and marks. A knot is a **record**: how many turns, which direction, what was tied inside. The player reads the pattern and reproduces it.

Knot and pattern puzzles are tactile and visual. They reward careful looking and simple deduction.

## Surfaces

| Surface | Where | What the player does |
|---|---|---|
| **Rope knot** | Cellar, attic, boathouse | Count turns and direction; reproduce the knot |
| **Tapestry** | Ballroom, guest room | Read the weave pattern as a sequence |
| **Antler branches** | Chapel, garden | Follow the branching order |
| **Sheet music** | Ballroom, study | Read repeated notes as a code |
| **Hair ribbon** | Guest room, body | Unbraid the pattern to reveal a message |

## Example — easy

A rope hangs in the cellar with four knots. Each knot has a different number of turns: 1, 2, 3, 4. A plaque reads:

> "The first turn opens the first door."

The player ties the first knot's pattern into a rope loop on the door. The door opens.

## Example — medium

A tapestry in the ballroom shows a repeating pattern of antlers: 2 branches, 4 branches, 3 branches, 2 branches, 4 branches. The pattern repeats three times, but one repeat is missing a branch. The player finds the missing branch count (3) and enters it on the wall panel.

## Example — hard

A sheet of music has three repeated notes circled. The notes correspond to numbers (A=1, B=2, C=3...). The player plays the notes on the piano in the order circled. The piano opens the second-floor seal.

## Solution logic

1. **Observe the pattern.** Count, direction, repetition, or branching.
2. **Find the rule.** A plaque, a song, a weaving draft, or a diagram.
3. **Reproduce or complete.** Tie the knot, fill the missing branch, play the notes, or enter the sequence.
4. **Confirm.** The mechanism responds.

## Item synergy

| Item | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Camera** | Captures the pattern for comparison. |
| **Magnifying Glass** | Reveals fine weave and tiny knots. |
| **Notebook** | Records the sequence and highlights the missing element. |
| **Compass** | Shows the direction of each knot's turn. |

## Integration

- **Clue:** A knot pattern hides the number of the room where the victim was staged.
- **Sigil:** The sigil is a rope knot; the player must reproduce the knot to break it.
- **Seal:** The attic seal is a tapestry pattern with one missing repeat.

## Accessibility

- All patterns have a text list (e.g., "2, 4, 3, 2, 4").
- The knot puzzle has a click-to-tie mode, not a physics challenge.
- The sheet music has a notation view and an audio playback.
- No color-only patterns.

## Design notes

- Knots should be **simple and readable**, not macrame.
- The pattern is a language: players learn to count branches and turns.
- The puzzle should take 45–90 seconds.
- The killer can cut a rope, but cannot change the pattern; the pattern is server-generated.
