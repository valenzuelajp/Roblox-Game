# Ghost Blueprint

> [!spoiler] Design spoiler
> The Ghost Blueprint is a Task 4 reward and a story reveal. Do not put it in the player wiki.

## What it is

The **Ghost Blueprint** is a rolled architectural drawing found in the Archive. It is dated **1926** and signed by an architect who died before the lodge was finished. It shows the house as it was **before** the chapel, the vault, and the east wing were added.

It is the reward for solving **Task 4 (Motive)**.

## What it reveals

| Reveal | Effect |
|---|---|
| **The original ritual entrance** | A sealed door in the cellar, behind the wine racks, that leads to the chapel crypt. |
| **The old secret passages** | Four passages that were walled up in 1936. |
| **The chapel's true orientation** | The altar faces the woods, not the road. |
| **The missing room** | A room on the 1926 plan that does not exist in the current house. |
| **The architect's note** | "The house is older than the foundation. Do not dig below the third cellar." |

The blueprint is **not** a map of the current layout. It shows the original house. The current house has changed. That difference is the clue.

## How it is used

1. **Found:** in the Archive, inside a map tube, behind the 1936 file.
2. **Claimed:** the first player to interact gets the blueprint (team-shared).
3. **Viewed:** opens a blueprint overlay. The player can rotate, zoom, and compare it to the current map.
4. **Marked:** the ritual entrance is highlighted; the old secret passages are outlined.
5. **Persistent:** the blueprint stays in the team's evidence board for the rest of the match.

The blueprint does **not** show:

- The current clue containers.
- The killer's staging room.
- The second pistol's seals.
- The Hollow Word.
- The killer's identity.

## The ritual entrance

The original ritual entrance is a **sealed door** in the cellar, behind the wine racks. It leads to the chapel crypt.

- Before the blueprint, the door is invisible: a wall of brick with no seam.
- After the blueprint, the door is visible to the team as a faint outline.
- The door can be opened with a **lockpick** (one charge) or by solving a **rune alignment** puzzle.
- The door is an **alternate route** to the chapel, not the only route.
- The killer can use it too.

## The old secret passages

The blueprint reveals four 1926 passages:

- **Library → Study** (behind the bookcase).
- **Kitchen → Cellar** (the dumbwaiter).
- **Ballroom → Chapel crypt** (behind the mirror).
- **Attic → Archive** (the chimney shaft).

These passages are **walled up** in the current house. The blueprint shows where they were, not where they are. A player can break through one with a **lockpick** or a **puzzle**, opening a new route.

The current seed's secret passages are separate. The blueprint does not reveal them.

## The missing room

The 1926 plan shows a room on the first floor that does not exist in the current house. The room is labeled **"The Ninth Guest."**

- The room is behind the archive wall.
- It can be opened with the **Ghost Blueprint** and a **rune alignment** puzzle.
- Inside: a small table, nine chairs, and a tenth chair with a rope.
- It contains a **story clue**, not a Case File clue: the names of the nine guests, carved into the table.

This room is optional. It is the deepest lore reward in the game.

## Story meaning

The blueprint is the house's **first lie**:

- The lodge was built over something older.
- The chapel was added later, facing the woods.
- The east wing was built to hide the crypt.
- The 1926 architect knew, and tried to warn the owner.
- Elias's family has been building the same house for a hundred years.

The blueprint is the moment the investigators realize the crime is not a murder. It is a **renovation**.

## UI and art

- The blueprint is a **cyanotype**: white lines on blue paper, cracked at the folds.
- It is viewed on a drafting table, not a full-screen menu.
- The player can pin it to the evidence board.
- The ritual entrance glows faintly when the player is in the cellar.
- The missing room is a blank rectangle with a single handwritten label.

## Accessibility

- The blueprint has a text summary: "The 1926 plan shows a sealed door behind the wine racks, four walled-up passages, and a room labeled The Ninth Guest."
- The overlay can be navigated with keyboard/gamepad.
- The ritual entrance has an audio cue (a low hum) when nearby.
- The missing room's label is readable in text.

## Design notes

- **The blueprint is a reward for solving the case, not a key to the case.**
- **It should make the player want to re-read the house.**
- **It should not trivialize navigation.** It shows the old house, not the new one.
- **The missing room is the story's best secret.** Do not put it in the player wiki.
